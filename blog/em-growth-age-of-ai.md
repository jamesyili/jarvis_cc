# How Engineering Managers Can Grow and Improve in the Age of AI

The role is changing faster than most EMs realize. Here's how to stay ahead.

## What to cover
- What AI is actually changing about EM work (not the hype version)
- The tools I use daily: Claude Code as chief of staff, knowledge base, coaching supplement
- How to use AI to get better at the parts of management that matter: decision-making, communication, stakeholder influence
- The trap: building AI systems as avoidance of the hard people work
- What can't be automated: judgment, trust, reading the room, emotional regulation
- Practical framework for EMs who want to be AI-native without losing what makes them effective leaders

## Why I'm writing this
This is my lived experience. G2 (AI-native capabilities) + G4 (executive presence). Also a unique perspective that few EMs are articulating publicly.
