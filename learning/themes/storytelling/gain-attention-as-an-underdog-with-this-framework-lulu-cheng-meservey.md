## Cultural erogenous zones: meeting audience where they already care

Lenny (00:14:40): You told me about that framework and I definitely wanted to hear more about it. What does that mean and is there something that you've seen someone do that's just like, 'Wow, they really nailed this erogenous zone approach.'

Lulu Cheng Meservey (00:14:54): Yeah, they really got it in the erogenous bull bullseye. So what it means is people have things that they either care about or don't, and you're not going to change that. So it's a huge lift to try to change someone's worldview or their passions. It's a light lift to take the thing you want to talk about and just shape it into, to fit into their worldview or their passions. There's not always a fit.

(00:15:26): There's going to be people who are just not your natural audience, and you should know that and not waste your time.
