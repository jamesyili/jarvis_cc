## Working backwards from problem, not from AI hype

Lenny (15:11): Is there an example of that that comes to mind of someone that did that incorrectly or wasted a lot of time?

Inbal Shani (15:17): It's not necessarily examples, but I've been talking to some of our customers and they're coming to us. "We heard about AI. What do you think we should do with AI?
