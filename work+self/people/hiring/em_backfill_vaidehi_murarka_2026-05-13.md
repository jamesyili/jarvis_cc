## What I'd want to see at onsite

To convert Lean Yes → Yes:

1. **A "no" story.** *"Time you said no to a stakeholder, or held the line on team scope when the org wanted you to take more on. What was the cost. What did you protect."*

5. **Direct conversation on growth expectations.** *"What would tell you in year 1 that Pinterest is the right place. What timeline are you calibrating against for the next level."* Either at onsite or recruiter — this needs to surface before offer, not after.
6. **PM partnership probe** — assign Tim or another senior PM to the loop. The 30–40 engineer ESR surface she described should mean she has real PM partnership reps. Test whether they look like the partnership shape we need.
