### The 15-minute sponsor ask (script shape, per Ethan)

Sponsor meetings are not "catch up." They're 4-step:

1. **Thesis in 20s** — "I'm driving Anticipation Foundations × Retentive Recs into an org-level capability: X because Y."
2. **Receipt in 20s** — "Proved via A/B/C; here's the artifact."
3. **Their win in 20s** — "Reduces your pain on ___ / accelerates ___."
4. **Precise ask** — pick ONE per meeting:
   - Pressure-test the thesis ("what would make you repeat it?")
   - VP altitude plug-in ("if this lands, where would you want me plugged in?")
   - Alignment leader ("who's the one leader I should align with so this doesn't die in handoffs?")
   - **"Can I use your name when I socialize this?" — the sponsorship litmus test**

### Orchestrating "opportunities flowing in" (per Ethan)
