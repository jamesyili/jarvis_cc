## The coverage design (8/24–9/11) — "is it basically just my bandwidth?"

**Verdict: ~80% yes.** With the Q3 freeze standing, Yang out of the picture, and the list above closed by 8/21, the *decision* load during the window is near zero **by design** — that's what the pre-departure list is for. But "basically bandwidth" undersells three things:

**1. It collides with the 8/1 law's first three weeks.** 8/24–9/11 is exactly when James's time was supposed to collapse to Reflex + UPP. So the coverage is designed **pull, not push**:
- **No standing 1:1 inheritance.** Eight directs × 3 weeks of 1:1s would eat the Reflex window whole. Instead: **one optional weekly office-hours block**, framed by Daniel in his coverage announcement ("James is available Thursdays if you want time").
- DRIs front everything; the escalation line is written in the coverage map: SEV / people-emergency / perf → James immediately; everything else → DRI decides or waits.

- One planned mid-window check-in: **Balaji** (his window closes in September; the 8/21 holding message needs a pulse).

**2. It's an EVS-window presence job, not passive availability.** These are 3 of the ~6 weeks before the Sept/Oct survey. The goal: every Curation ML IC can still answer *what does my team own, how do I grow, who fights for me* with their EM gone. The instruments already exist — Daniel's cascade + coverage announcement before he leaves, the skip-levels (Yongwoo, Felix, Esteban), visible-but-light office hours. No new programs.

**3. It's a discipline job — the compact binds James too.** Three weeks of direct exposure to Daniel's team means James will *see* things: gaps, talent, misroutes, seams. The reorg-brain will want to act. Everything observed goes to the **settled-or-open ledger** for November — nothing structural moves in his absence (exception: safety/perf emergencies).
