## Room 1 — Trio sync, Kim segment (~7 min, inside the boards showcase)

**Objective:** socialize the updated direction at manager level, get Daniel's reaction in the room (not secondhand later), and protect the decision for Friday. This segment *informs* the boards discussion; it doesn't decide anything.

**The frame (say it in this order):**
1. **Lead with her voice, not the org design.** "I did a career deep-dive with Kim this week. Her energy is unambiguous: user-visible product work, spinning up new things, and she lit up specifically on Retentive Recs. She's already working the retrieval side via search-stage CLR — so this is continuity, not a switch."
2. **The seat concept, updated:** RR-weighted under Alim (supersedes the UEB-only exploration seat from the earlier boards), bulk RR + CLR-relevance help, UPP winding down per the standing Dhruvil agreement.
3. **The Chuxi guardrail, named out loud** (fold into the RR R&R segment — nobody else in the room carries her): "Chuxi remains workstream TL — her runway is a deliberate bet and it holds. Kim's seat, if it lands, is chartered as ownership of the explorative-pUIC technical spine — a complementary lane, not a competing TL claim. Kim herself was explicit she's not claiming TL."
4. **Keep Friday as the decision point:** "Direction points RR-under-Alim, but the decision lands Friday — after Alim and Kim have talked and after the three of us [James+Daniel+Kim] close the transition together."

**Daniel handling — invite the counter, don't dodge it.** His Balaji-for-RR read is on record; better it surfaces Monday than after. If he raises it (or proactively): "You'd floated Balaji for RR — I see them as different shapes, not competitors. Balaji is the systems-TL profile; Kim's lane is the explorative-pUIC spine with a product face. Both can be true, and the IB gate feeds where Balaji lands anyway. Let's carry both into Friday." Do not litigate Kim's level or record in this room — if Daniel's critical read (depth, impact) surfaces, altitude answer: "That's exactly the setup conversation I'm running with her — fit first, then the growth case on a clean charter." Then move on.

**Watch-fors:** (a) any drift toward deciding the seat in the room — pull it back to Friday; (b) IC-level leakage — nothing from this segment reaches ICs, double-weight with Alim's PA-trip Search dinners 8/25–27; (c) Alim enthusiasm running ahead — "hold the details for our 1:1, I have a fuller brief for you."

**Exit state:** both EMs leave knowing the direction and the process; Daniel feels consulted, not informed; Chuxi's protection is on the record in front of both; nothing decided.

## Room 2 — Alim 1:1 (after the trio, before his chat with Kim)

**Objective:** convert Alim from *willing to take Kim* to *signed up to sponsor Kim* — with the lane design agreed and his chat with her correctly framed. Enjoyment is the wrong bar; he'll clear it cheaply. The ask is sponsorship.

**Open with the gift frame:** "You told me you want seniors under you. This is that want being met — an L15 with a four-year record of turning ambiguity into systems, who multiplies the engineers around her, and who *chose* your space."

**The context package (all sanctioned):**
- Strengths pattern from her review history: creates direction from ambiguity, builds reusable foundations that outlast launches, strong operational judgment, multiplies engineers, exceptional written clarity.
- Her stated energy: product-visible impact, full-stack background, not-100%-modeling, loves ramping new areas to expertise and then teaching/documenting.
- Already on RR's retrieval challenge via search-stage CLR — continuity.
- Her one stated concern: no very-senior in-space person to learn from. Answer: Olafur (see wiring below).
- Her own carefulness: she explicitly won't claim TL because of Chuxi.

**The lane design to agree before he meets her:**
- **Chuxi = workstream TL.** Coordination, roadmap, the runway bet — intact. Alim holds this line personally; if it erodes, the reorg's TL bet dies quietly.
- **Kim = named owner of the explorative-pUIC spine.** Charter starts at retrieval (challenge #2, where she already is) and **explicitly grows into model ownership on challenge #1, with a metric she owns**. Ask him to put her name on that roadmap line, visibly. "Helps RR" is the failure mode — she'll deliver excellent infrastructure and her growth case stays open.
- **The trap to name for him:** his pod is junior and Kim is senior — the comfortable move is absorbing her as his de-facto systems TL / platform counterweight. That seat is the REQ-1/Balaji-shaped spec, *not* Kim: it would re-weight her toward backend, which is exactly the pattern her growth case needs to break. Her charter is the spine, not his counterweight.

**The sponsorship ask (the real ask — get specific commitments, not enthusiasm):**
"What failed her before wasn't performance, it was legibility — nobody named her ownership, nobody brought her into the forums, the work class she carried is invisible at calibration. I'm personally sponsoring the fix at calibration. I need you running the on-team half: her name on the charter publicly, her in the rooms where RR is discussed, and the two of us telling one consistent story about her scope." Then confirm each of the three concretely.

**Framing his chat with Kim:** it's mutual — she's choosing too. He's a new EM, remote, and she's had setups not work before; the chat is him *earning* her as much as meeting her. Register: curiosity about what energizes her, his vision for RR, what he'd personally do to set her up — not an assessment interview. Explicitly not a done deal in that conversation; the decision closes Friday.

**Olafur wiring:** her senior-anchor concern is real and he's the answer — IC17, Dylan-direct, Federation/FBL, exactly in-space — but only if the relationship is explicit. James makes the ask to Olafur directly (defined cadence: design reviews + standing unblock channel); ask Alim to wire Olafur into RR's design-review rhythm so he's structurally present, not a goodwill advisor.

**Exit state:** Alim has agreed to the Chuxi/Kim lane split, the challenge-#1 growth clause, and the three sponsorship behaviors; knows the chat framing; knows Friday closes it.
