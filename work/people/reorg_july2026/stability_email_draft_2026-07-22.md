**Subject:** Welcoming Curation ML to the CG org

Team,

Following Dylan's note, I wanted to add a few words directly. Org changes can raise questions even when the change is a good one, and this one is a good one.

First, to Daniel and the Curation ML team: I'm genuinely excited to welcome you. Your work on recommendation quality speaks for itself, and bringing it together with our retrieval work opens up a lot we've wanted to do jointly for a while. Thank you to Yan and the Personalized Experience team for their leadership of this work so far. We'll keep collaborating closely, especially on the curation work that spans both orgs.

I also want to be clear about what this means day to day, because stability matters to me. For now, nothing changes about your projects, your priorities, or who you work with. Daniel continues to lead Curation ML. We're bringing these groups together so we can do more together, not to disrupt what's already working.

Over the coming weeks I'll spend time understanding the full scope on both sides before proposing anything further. If we do make changes down the line, they'll come through a deliberate process, with your input, and with plenty of notice. I'd rather move thoughtfully and get it right than move fast and create churn, and I'll share where things stand at regular check-ins along the way.

Most of all, my commitment is to take care of everyone affected by this change. If you have questions or concerns, large or small, please bring them. We'll set up team sessions in the next few days to talk through all of it together.

Looking forward to what we'll build.

James
