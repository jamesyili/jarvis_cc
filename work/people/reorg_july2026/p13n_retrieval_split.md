## The five principles (James's tone: accountability over metrics · people first · long-term over quick hacks)

Each principle = design translation + a visible cost (a principle that costs nothing settles nothing).

1. **Accountability over metrics** — *every workstream has one named owner who answers for outcomes; metrics are evidence, not the boss.* One accountable EM + TL per workstream (POC table as principle); no diffuse co-ownership; experiment-ledger QC = inspectable accountability without meetings. Bite: owners explain failures — no hiding behind green dashboards, AND no excusing misses with narrative (must not be heard as "metrics optional" given Jeff's SSv2/cost mandates).
2. **People first** *(James's meaning: talent-first)* — *we know who our best people are and design the org so they're energized.* Talent placement = first-order design input; engine+bet pairing per PERSON, not just per team; standing EM-sync question: "who are your best people, what excites them now, what would make them leave?" Bite: investment follows talent, not tenure or fairness-optics — exciting problems are NOT spread evenly; less-glamorous work staffed honestly + made measurable. Tripwire it creates: a strong person going flat = design failure, one of the few things that can trigger change between decision points. (Also = Dylan's "learn to motivate people" coaching operationalized — let her see it in the Oct rationale. Quietly: the energized/carrying review surfaces underperformance as a byproduct — no separate register needed at this table.)
3. **Long-term investments over quick hacks** — *every team holds a reliable gains engine AND real 0-to-1 bets; no all-risk or all-harvest team.* Portfolio frame becomes stated design law; bets get explicit room to be non-green early (Dylan's RR/offsite-signals air-cover pattern, made policy); compounding work (substrate, frameworks, publishing) counts as output. Bite: we ship slower than a hack would allow and defend bets through ugly early readouts.
4. **No surprises** — *people follow their project; moves come with conversation + notice; span exists so everyone gets real manager attention.* Continuity default when units move; nobody learns of their own change second-hand; a less elegant chart beats a broken growth path. (Renamed 8/1 — was drafted as "people first" before James defined that as talent-first.)
5. **Evidence-gated evolution** — *structural changes fire when pre-agreed criteria fire — not advocacy, timing pressure, or who argued best last sync.* IB moves on gains-origin; GenRet on graduation criteria; reqs shaped by demonstrated load. This is what makes principles-first enforceable — and what lets the private constraints bind invisibly as criteria that haven't fired.
   - **Comms discipline (rider on #5): nothing leaves the table as a maybe.** EMs don't preview scenarios to ICs; teams hear decisions, with notice, from their manager.
