### 6.3 The nine ground rules (final, in the primer)

1. **First Team** — this table over your own team; conflicts get said and settled here.
2. **We cut once** — observe through fall; ~November calls on pre-agreed criteria; then structure stands. Stability promise attaches to the settle, not the interim.
3. **Every team gets a scoreboard, a flag, and its share of the plumbing** — metric goals (engagement, retention, cost), a known-for-something-in-a-year mission, explicit org obligations.
4. **We start from strength** — standing weekly question: who are our best people, what energizes them, what would make them leave?
5. **No ceilings from us on our best people** — "remove every limit that's mine to remove, fight the ones that aren't." (Deliberately NOT a comp guarantee.)
6. **Decision rights, said once** — final calls are James's; honest reads + disagree-and-commit.
7. **Nothing leaves this table as a maybe** — teams hear decisions from their own manager, with notice; never scenarios.
8. **AI-first operations** — Reflex as program bet, PINvestigator + Pinkerton live examples, Daniel's Pin tools named as the same pattern (deliberate: hands Daniel the proven-authority seat on this rule).
9. **We don't fund everything — because we can't** — selective and decisive; explicit no's with reasons, never slow starvation.
