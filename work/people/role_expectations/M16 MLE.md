JOB TITLE
M16 - Manager II, Machine Learning Engineering
Scope of Role
- Independently leads a team
- Typically provides leadership and direction through senior ICs
- Plans over a 6-12 month time horizon
- Area of ownership: A broad set of features within a single workstream/domain, or multiple services, or a large platform or functional roadmap impacting multiple cross-functional team initiatives
PINTEREST COMPETENCIES


Customer Focus
Building strong Pinners, partners, and/or stakeholders relationships and delivering user-centric solutions.
Identifies appropriate functional resources and support needed to meet current and emerging needs of Pinners, partners, and/or stakeholders; solicits user feedback on behalf of the team to understand problems and potential solutions.
Being Resilient
Rebounding from setbacks and adversity when facing difficult situations.
Remains objective and calm while taking a forward-thinking approach when faced with adversity. Grows from hardships, difficult experiences, and setbacks. Manages crises and volatile situations effectively.
Courage
Stepping up to address difficult issues; saying what needs to be said.
Confronts tough organizational issues and disagreements, delivering difficult messages directly and saying "no" when necessary.
Ensures Accountability
Holding self and others accountable for meeting commitments.
Identifies and monitors metrics and milestones for projects or products one leads. Designs feedback processes into the work. Holds self accountable for outcomes.
Decision Quality
Making good and timely decisions that keep Pinterest moving forward.
Promptly makes sound decisions while seeking appropriate context and vetting better alternatives when necessary. Synthesizes information, experience, and various inputs to determine the best course of action. Willing to make tough decisions, even if unpopular, after considering all relevant inputs and alternative courses of action.
Values Differences
Recognizing the value that different perspectives and backgrounds bring to an organization.
Interacts with others in ways that is sensitive to norms and expectations. Encourages others to stay open to, seek, and learn from different perspectives. Actively seeks out information and activities centered around a wide range of backgrounds, and viewpoints.
PINTEREST MANAGER COMPETENCIES


Manages Ambiguity
Operating effectively, even when things are not certain or the way forward is not clear.
Shows sufficient comfort with ambiguity and change; resolves escalations and keeps people and projects moving forward. Coaches direct reports in navigating ambiguity, finding solutions and accomplishing goals despite incomplete information. Embraces ambiguity and change; recognizes their role as a steward of business-driven changes and helps the team adapt, remain productive and move forward.	
Develops Talent
Developing people to meet both their career goals and the organization’s goals, including promoting fairness and inclusion.
Offers real-time, constructive feedback and coaching on how others can achieve team objectives. Fosters a psychologically safe culture for sharing and receiving feedback across a diverse team. Equitably supports the developmental efforts of team members and thought partners on developmental opportunities. Develops others through coaching, feedback, exposure, and stretch assignments.
Drives Results
Consistently achieving results, even under tough circumstances.
Encourages and enables a high performance team culture; challenges poor outcomes or unproductive behaviors for self and others. Shows determination in the face of obstacles and setbacks. Sets high standards for own and team performance and results; consistently delivers tasks to successful completion.
RESPONSIBILITIES


Team Impact and Metrics

Sets challenging but achievable OKRs and adjusts OKRs based on progress and business changes. Connects business goals to technical deliverables.
1. Sets challenging but achievable team OKRs aligned with company strategy in collaboration with cross-functional partners. Adjusts OKRs based on progress and business changes.
2. Connects team OKRs to technical deliverables, develops methods for and evaluates progress of the total product against its success metrics, and drives subsequent development iteration and decisions based on data.
3. Accountable for the team’s delivery (and possibly shared delivery with partner teams) of key results on time and within budget. May share delivery accountability with partner teams as well.
Technical Excellence

Defines and upholds the technical and quality bar for the team, applies technical expertise to steer projects and identify roadmap opportunities, and maintains sustainable services, systems, and processes.
1. Defines and upholds the technical bar for the quality, accuracy, and timeliness of the team's output.
2. Understands, defines, and communicates technical requirements, project goals, and output artifacts. Sets clear expectations of priorities, deliverables, and exit criteria.
3. Applies technical expertise and relevant data to identify opportunities that shape the cross-functional roadmap.
4. Team’s systems have high availability with sustainable KTLO. Ensures bugs and incident remediations are resolved in a timely manner, while investing in team agility. Identifies systemic failures (recurring bugs or incidents) and prioritizes work to correct them.


Technical Talent Development

Aligns technical projects and resources with their team's capabilities and career goals. Grows the team's technical and leadership capabilities through mentorship and top talent recruitment.
1. Creates equitable growth opportunities for their team and aligns people and roles to maximize value for team objectives and people
2. Understands and identifies technical skill set gaps within their team, recruits top technical talent for open roles, and plans for future attrition within their team. Helps calibrate recruiting efforts and closes senior candidates.
3. Grows and supports technical, decision-making, ownership, leadership, and technical mentorship capabilities across the team.

PEOPLE MANAGER SKILLS


Leadership and Direction
Inspires and guides others, communicating actions needed and how they relate to broader organizational mission and strategy.
Adapts strategies and priorities to the team level to help set expectations and clarify actions needed. Explains the relationship to the broader organization's mission, vision and values; motivates the team to commit to these priorities and aim for extraordinary to achieve local business goals.
Resourcing & Workforce Planning
Ensures the organization can recruit people with the skills it needs to achieve competitive advantage, while also balancing its resources in order to meet short-term and long-term strategic goals.
With manager guidance, works to ensure the team has the resources now and in the near term and can recruit people with the skills to deliver results towards short- and long-term strategic goals.
Performance Management
Sets work objectives for self and others and appropriately manages the work to achieve them. Holds self and others accountable. Takes corrective action when needed, using Pinterest performance management systems and tools.
Manages team performance, setting appropriate performance expectations for each team member and holding them accountable for achieving them; provides developmental feedback to ensure the achievement of team objectives; proactively addresses and resolves low performance. Accountable for formal performance management and reviews of direct reports.
Organizational Capability Building
Develops capabilities at a team or organizational level to ensure they are relevant to current and future organizational objectives and enable the organization at large to achieve business goals and fulfill the potential of its people.
Leverages Pinterest role expectations resources and tools to identify team members' individual development needs. Identifies actions and opportunities for team members to build capabilities and foster professional growth. Provide informal training or coaching to others throughout the organization in own area of expertise to enable others to improve performance and fulfill personal potential.
FUNCTIONAL SKILLS


Strategic Thinking and Technical Alignment

Identifies what’s critical for the team’s success and aligns functional deliverables with organizational or company goals.
Evaluates business and technical factors and makes well informed decisions that guide technical and product investments and innovations and yield successful outcomes.
1. Defines and builds alignment around a strategic technical vision that connects deliverables for a team or group of teams to platform or organizational goals.
2. Breaks customer or business needs into specific technical deliverables and works with peers to complete cross-team or cross-functional projects beyond their immediate ownership.
3. Builds a team investment portfolio across time horizons and risk, including aggressive bets on new concepts, features, designs, or technologies
4. Raises the technical bar by supporting and challenging engineers as they produce designs and implementations, and builds team members into technical leads that are also responsible for bar-raising.
Collaboration and Risk Management

Communicates team strategy and progress to stakeholders and actively manages risks to ensure the team’s success, including escalating issues when appropriate.
Builds partnerships with cross-functional stakeholders and engineering leaders to influence outcomes and build consensus.
1. Communicates with clarity, brevity, and focus, and tailors their message to an audience of their function, cross-functional partners, and internal consumers
2. Collaborates with and influences team members and project stakeholders to build consensus and alignment across within a scoped project, across teams areas
3. Identifies and resolves misalignment within a scoped project, across teams. Escalates and raises broad project, multiple-team, or cross-functional alignment issues.
4. Proactively identifies, mitigates, and drives resolution of risks to teams' objectives. Adopts a fail fast mentality, optimizing for experimentation and gathering learnings upfront
5. Effectively communicates the team’s strategy, its alignment with the company's objectives, as well as status, risks, and tradeoffs to the appropriate audiences
Team Execution and Technical Outcomes

Drives execution and velocity, balancing feature and product work with technical investments.
Delegates tactical decision making and builds a culture of ownership as they scale their leadership.
1. Drives execution by organizing team work, setting goals, and holding the team accountable. Independently unblocks the team when necessary to ensure progress is made.
2. Creates process and structure to track and enable the delivery of the team's projects. Empowers team members to improve processes or structures. Fosters a culture of continuous improvement within the team.
3. Monitors velocity and actively utilizes project management tactics to accelerate timelines and mitigate risks. Re-prioritizes investments in response to new information and makes cuts when needed.
4. Delegates tasks within the team, tailoring assignments both to ensure effective technical delivery and to provide growth opportunities for individuals. At times shares the role of tactical decision-maker with leads within the team.
5. Ensures the team holds a high bar for technical execution via robust processes (ex: for reviews, monitoring, alerting, incident prevention and response, bug SLAs, and quality control) and pays down technical debt.
Technical Judgment

Demonstrates technical expertise in their domain, with the ability to drive technical approaches, designs, strategy, and decision-making.
1. Demonstrates in-depth technical expertise in the domain of their team and high-level knowledge of the technical domains of the teams they work with.
2. Sets implementation priorities within the team, or adjacent areas balancing impact, agility, quality, and sustainability. May review technical artifacts (such as critical PRs, analyses, or technical design documents) depending on technical strengths and gaps within the team.
3. Understands and can communicate decisions and tradeoffs in technical components (ex: architectural design or statistical methodology) to peers, partners, and stakeholders. Guides and facilitates implementation-level technical decision making.
4. Provides actionable feedback on technical artifacts, identifying potential issues with implementation, and building alignment on goals, metrics, and use-cases with a focus on sustainability and simplicity
5. Holds themselves and senior team members accountable for the overall technical roadmap
6. Understands and leverages available technologies or frameworks to increase velocity or quality, and shares reusable solutions with peers
7. Empowers senior ICs to drive components of the roadmap (technical designs, analysis, etc)
