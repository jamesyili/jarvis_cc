# Daniel 1:1 — The Open (first conversation post-announcement)

**For:** James · **When:** same day as / right after Dylan's reorg announcement · **Type:** listening-first, relationship-opening — *not* a charter briefing.

**Dylan's actual mandate for this conversation:** assess Daniel's **retention** and **learn what motivates him** (she pre-confirmed: LLM-based work excites him). So your #1 job is to *listen*, not to structure.

---

## The frame to hold the entire time

- **Scope follows people.** He and his team come to you as a unit; nothing is being pulled apart.
- **Minimal change now.** Team stays intact, he keeps leading it, the only mechanical change is the reporting line (Yann → you). Lateral, *not* a layer dropped on him.
- **Opportunity, not reassignment.** This is his opening to get more involved over time in the direction he wants — plus **things you build together** later. Something to *work on*, not change today.
- **Talk less than half the time.** If you're doing most of the talking, you're doing it wrong.

---

## The open (what you actually say — then hand him the floor)

> "Daniel — I'm genuinely glad you and the team are joining. Before I say anything about structure, let me get the structure question out of the way, because the honest answer is: **not much changes day one.** Your team stays your team, you keep leading it, and the only real change is you report to me now instead of Yann. I'm not here to reorganize you or take your people.
>
> So what I actually want out of this conversation isn't to tell you a plan — it's to understand how *you're* thinking about this, and what you want. You've been doing this a while; I'd rather build the next chapter around what energizes you than hand you a box on a chart. Let me start by listening."

Then stop talking and move to the questions.

---

## Listen (the core — this is the assignment)

Open-ended first, let him run, then sharpen:

1. **What work energizes you most right now — where do you *want* your technical time and your team's going?** *(Does "LLM / generative" come out unprompted? That tells you how real Dylan's read is.)*
2. **If you could shape your team's charter for the next year, what would it be?**
3. **What's felt blocked or underleveraged lately?** *(Lets the "underutilized" feeling surface from him — you never impose it.)*
4. **What would make this move a clear win for you? What would make it a bad outcome?**
5. **Where do you want to grow — what does the next level look like for you?**
6. **Who on your team is critical or a flight risk, and what do they need?**

**Flight-risk watch-fors:** flat affect on the charter; "I need to think about it"; steering to level/comp early; strong attachment to Yann; treating boards as a dead end; fishing about external optionality. Two+ → flag to Dylan and accelerate the future-collaboration path.

## Landmine scripts (only if he raises them — don't pre-open)

| He asks / probes | Your stance |
|---|---|
| **Is my team staying intact, or are you breaking it up?** | "Day-one intent is your team stays whole. Anything structural down the line is deliberate and you're in the room before it moves — I'm not reshuffling you now." *(Don't promise "never." Don't pre-announce the RR→Alim shed.)* |
| **Will I get to work on LLM / generative modeling?** | Honest: "Not by leading Retentive Recs or Reflex — but there's real room to grow into the generative direction over time, and there's work there I'd genuinely want to build *with* you." *(Trajectory + partnership, not a charter promise.)* |
| **Am I capped reporting into another Senior EM?** | "No. Frontier boards + scoring modeling with a real metric surface and a publication track is a Senior-EM-track story, and I'll sponsor it." |
| **Do I get headcount for this?** | Honest: "Not this cycle — the headcount this round is narrow. The *scope* is the growth right now, and I'll carry the case for more next cycle." |
| **What's the Yann situation — am I leaving on good terms?** | Grace, no drama: "Good terms all around. This is about putting your modeling work where it has the most surface, not about Yann." |
| **Why me — is this a place things got parked?** | "The opposite. Your modeling work becomes more central here, not less." |

---

## Don'ts

- **Don't pitch a charter.** You're listening, not selling.
- **Don't over-promise an LLM leg** to retain him — Anna's veto (RR) and the Reflex context problem make that a promise you'd walk back.
- **Don't say "underutilized."** It's Dylan's private read, not his narrative.
- **Don't promise permanent team structure.** Promise *process* (you hear it from me first), not *outcome*.
- **Don't trash the past or Yann.**

---

## Close the open + set the next beat

- **Genuine hook (ties to your own homework):** "I've been digging into the notif launch review — that collaboration is exactly the kind of win I want more of. I'd love you to walk me through how your team thinks about that surface." *(Signals you value the work + you're doing the reading, not just managing from altitude.)*
- **Set the cadence:** regular 1:1s starting now; a proper follow-up once you've both had a couple weeks to see how things settle.
- **Leave him with the frame:** "Nothing changes this week except that I'm now the person in your corner. Let's use the next stretch for me to learn your world before either of us decides anything."
