## The three reasons

### Wishy-washy

Sustained pattern, not a one-off. The disagreement-with-manager example concluded in middle ground. The trust-with-stronger-TL example concluded in shared ideation. The 90-day plan concluded in "let's see." Every answer found a way to soften into collaboration before landing a position. The seat needs an EM who will say no to surface teams, hold Charlie's case under HR pressure, and defend JJ's calibration packet against pushback. None of that surfaced. He defaulted to the middle every time he had a choice.

### No energy

Flat across the hour. The auto research story — Karpathy tweet to a 13% recall lift in a week — should have been the moment the energy spiked. It didn't. He described it the same way he described the round-tables and the skills matrix. If a 13% lift doesn't bring energy in an interview, the day-to-day grind of CG (regressions, calibration, surface fires, perf cases) is going to be a slog. Dylan reads energy. Rajat reads energy. The seat needs someone who shows up at a higher voltage than this.

### No specific details

- **90-day plan:** three philosophy buckets (people / project / innovation). Zero named moves mapped to specific weeks. No mention of OKR review, allocation lens, PSC packet equivalent, named TLs, named priorities. Vaidehi gave concrete moves under each phase unprompted; he didn't even when explicitly asked what he'd want me and his skip to *see* at the end of 90 days.
- **Perf case:** named the candidate as "L5 who didn't want to step up to L6" — Amazon up-or-out structural framing, not output-quality. No metric, no specific behavioral signal, no named coaching artifact, no timeline. PIP → left, end of story.
- **Stronger-TL example:** answered the wrong question. Question was about a senior TL technically deeper than him; answer was about a former Science Manager he inherited as IC. Different power dynamic. He had positional authority over the demoted person; the asked-for scenario was the inverse.
- **Hiring strategy:** got as far as the skills matrix and stopped. No sourcing strategy. No signal beyond self-evaluation. No bar. No loop design. No named recent hire as evidence the muscle exists.
- **Promo-advocacy:** never surfaced. Not asked-and-deflected — never came up at all, including in his own people-management answer where it should have lived next to the perf case.

He showed soft collaboration, vague structure, and no voltage. Eight years of domain tenure does not compensate for the gap on the four muscles that actually run this seat.
