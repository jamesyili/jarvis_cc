The question explicitly named *"active performance case and a promo candidate."* He answered:
- ✅ How to map work + components
- ✅ How to map team ownership
- ✅ How to find ROI gaps (good StockX example)
- ❌ Did NOT address handling the active perf case

He answered the technical/operational half and avoided the people/political half. Senior EMs at this altitude *lean into* the people-and-politics half — that's where their value lives.

### Q4 — Biggest red flag
Asked a *behavioral* question: "How do you earn trust from a senior TL who's deeper technically than you? Give me a specific 60-day example."

**He never gave the specific 60-day example.** Instead:
- Pivoted to a long technical architecture overview (FIT model, L1, multi-tools, embeddings, two-tower, token-based, dynamic prompts, RAG)
- Then a generic empowerment framework

When he heard "FIT model, L1," he interpreted it as a request to demonstrate technical competence, not as context for the relational question. That's an IC instinct under pressure, not an EM instinct.

This confusion — "show me how you handle a specific person" → "let me show you what I know technically" — is exactly what doesn't scale at Pinterest senior EM.
