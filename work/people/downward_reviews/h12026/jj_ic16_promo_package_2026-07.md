## Q1 — Describe the business need and scope expansion that require a role at the next level. Consider how the role's ownership, influence, complexity and/or decision making authority has expanded to meet the business need.

The P13N Candidate Generation team owns the early funnel of Pinterest's HF and BMI surfaces, and its problems sit squarely at the intersection of ML systems and ML models: candidate generation, lightweight scoring, serving architecture, caching, latency, and cost, alongside the models they serve. Wins here move successful sessions, repins, and retention directly. For this Staff MLE role on the team, there two strong business needs.

The first is foundations and efficiency. As we continue to modernize the early funnel from many heuristics CG and a simple L1 model to few ML-based CG and a more sophisticated L1 model + unified utility layer, we need to be able to do this through sustained cost and latency efficiency, such as:

- adding new optimization opportunities and business controls in l1 utility
- adding in-session responsiveness cost-effectively (caching, invalidation)
- introduction and continued support of critical recommendation system feature such as pin selection, responsive signals, trust and safety filters, and
- legacy CG and system deprecations.

The second driving business need is continued investment in AI tooling and systems work, such as:

- Reflex Build Agents to increase agentic engineering productivity and automate experiment set up
- Pinvestigator to run automated metric debugging to save engineering time
- Pinkerton to facilitate greater experimental understanding and dsat investigations

The work in these scopes have amounted to cost savings worth >3 millions of dollars annually to help fund ML improvements in this space as well as dev velocity increases. We expect the scope of work to continue. Because both of these scopes straddles systems and the models they serve, this critical role requires a Staff level technical lead who is adept at both systems and ML. It will also require someone who can own ambitious technical strategy across team boundaries, influences partner teams' roadmaps, and coordinates across more than > 10 engineers across several teams, including directly leading at least 3 from the P13N CG team.

## Q3 — What areas does this employee need to develop to be successful at the next level?

One area for JJ to develop is decision ownership, and building the associated communication skills to land those decisions well with those involved. JJ's technical judgment is strong and is rarely the limiting factor. The pattern to grow past is that he often arrives with sound analysis and well framed options, then routes the final call to others or to group consensus for ratification.

Feedback from Dafang named the same theme, asking for "more leadership in decision making," specifically being the one to make hard calls, such as killing directions that are not working, rather than facilitating options for others to choose.

At the Staff level, the expectations for JJ would be to have a strong technical point of view, and can bring others around a decision point ("here is what we are doing and why, push back if you disagree") and reserving escalation for the truly irreversible or cross cutting calls.
