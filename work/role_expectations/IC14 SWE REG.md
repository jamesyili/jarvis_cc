JOB TITLE
IC14 - Software Engineer II
Role Summary
A Software Engineer 2 requires minimal technical guidance in order to independently contribute to projects and deliver solutions on a daily basis.
SCOPE OF ROLE


Scope
- Works on business or technical problems of moderate scope and ambiguity which are often varied and routine where analysis of situations or data requires a review of a variety of factors.
- Applies learned techniques and contributes to analysis and investigation to solve problems.
- Work is delivered mostly independently with some guidance from others, and is reviewed at critical points.
- Normally receives little instruction on day-to-day work, general instructions on new assignments.
Level of Independence
Demonstrates an increasing level of independence in software development tasks, occasionally seeking support when faced with challenges or complexities.
PINTEREST COMPETENCIES


Customer Focus
Building strong Pinners, partners, and/or stakeholders relationships and delivering user-centric solutions.
Solicits feedback from Pinners, partners, and/or stakeholders and searches for ways to improve the customer experience; shows a clear understanding of the team's ability to use Pinner input to continuously improve.
Being Resilient
Rebounding from setbacks and adversity when facing difficult situations.
Demonstrates composure and professionalism under difficult circumstances; recovers quickly from problems and setbacks; maintains productivity under stress or pressure, overcoming obstacles and learning from failures. 	
Courage
Stepping up to address difficult issues; saying what needs to be said.
Addresses tough team issues and disagreements by identifying the problem, building consensus, and reaching resolution, regardless of potential criticism.
Ensures Accountability
Holding self and others accountable for meeting commitments.
Advances responsibility for work product and performance, recognizing when to change approaches; moves problems toward resolution and delivers on commitments, seeking input when encountering ambiguity.
Decision Quality
Making good and timely decisions that keep Pinterest moving forward.
Demonstrates good judgment in routine, day-to-day decision making, determining whether to act independently or to escalate an issue to ensure quality outcomes. 	
Values Differences
Recognizing the value that different perspectives and cultures bring to an organization.
Behaves with sensitivity toward differences in cultural norms, expectations, and ways of communicating to foster belonging; seeks out and understands the value of diverse perspectives, backgrounds and talents of others. Works effectively with others who have different perspectives, backgrounds, and/or styles.
RESPONSIBILITIES


Impact
Delivers impact through hands-on technical work and has a track record of completing tasks, projects, etc that have impact to team and project goals.
- Hands-on technical work is the primary measure of impact.
- Has a track record of successfully meeting project goals.
- Contributes to meaningful team-level initiatives as a member of project teams.
- Leads team-level initiatives of limited impact and scope, such as minor feature iterations, code improvements, and/or dependency upgrades.
Technical Excellence
Delivers production-ready solutions, follows and adapts best practices, and holds a high bar for technical quality.
- Delivers production-ready code with minor help from code reviewers.
- Trusted code reviewer.
- Follows and contributes to the evolution of documented best practices for testing and validating code changes.
Service/Feature Quality and Availability
Maintains healthy and reliable systems, fixes assigned bugs within defined SLAs, and effectively contributes to on-call rotations
- Takes responsibility for maintaining the team's systems, improving reliability and health, fixing issues that block team's deliverables, and improving Engineering Quality.
- If the team has an on-call rotation, they will typically be a member of this rotation.
- Can independently address production issues, bugs and defects, but requires some assistance in a limited number of more complex scenarios.
- Updates service documentation, tooling and run books as the service evolves over time.
- Fixes assigned bugs within defined SLAs. Takes proactive steps to increase automated test coverage to avoid recurrence of bugs.
Mentorship and Knowledge Sharing
Uplevels the team through mentorship, feedback, and knowledge sharing
- Takes proactive measures to mentor junior team members on an as-needed basis.
- Contributes to and maintains team level technical documentation.
- Participates in and presents at technical presentations such as brown bags, all hands, technical design/analysis reviews.
Engineering Citizenship
See Engineering Citzenship document
FUNCTIONAL SKILLS


Software Design and Architecture
Leverages software design and architecture best practices and writes clear technical design documents to build alignment and ensure successful execution
- Independently emulates the style and architecture of the existing code base.
- Writes clear technical design documents for own projects with some help from reviewers.
- Brings ideas to team discussions that may need some refinement.
Efficient Execution
Drives succesful execution of projects through milestone setting, anticipating risks/uncertainties, and making improvements to team processes
- Independently executes on an unambiguous project with some support and guardrails.
- Sets milestones, breaks up tasks and runs retros with a little support.
- Presents the technical design or project progress to the team with some help.
- Shares their opinion on existing processes and/or practices of the team, to improve team execution
Communication and Alignment
Effectively and proactively communicates to ensure alignment, and tailors their message to the audience
- Communicates with clarity, brevity, and focus, and tailors their message to an audience of their function, and project cross-functional partners
- Actively listens to others and asks clarifying questions. Effectively participates in team discussions, and often brings topics to discuss with the team
- Proactively communicates relevant information and status of own work to the team and to stakeholders.
