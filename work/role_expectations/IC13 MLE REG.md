JOB TITLE
IC13 - Machine Learning Engineer I
Role Summary
A Machine Learning Engineer 1 can solve clearly scoped problems with guidance. This role requires a background in computer science: typically an apprenticeship, internship and/or a college degree. This role is as much about learning as it is about doing.
SCOPE OF ROLE


Scope
Works on well-defined problems or makes improvements on production ML systems by following a well-defined process.

Examples:
- Adding signals (already available offline & online) to a ranking model, training the model, evaluating the model offline and conducting an online A/B experiment
- Integrating an open-source ML library into a training pipeline and following the team’s testing process to ensure the functionality provided by the open-source library works correctly.
Level of Independence
Is able to successfully complete tasks under the guidance and supervision of others.
PINTEREST COMPETENCIES


Customer Focus
Building strong Pinners, partners, and/or stakeholders relationships and delivering user-centric solutions.
Solicits feedback from Pinners, partners, and/or stakeholders and searches for ways to improve the customer experience; develops a fundamental understanding of the team's ability to address input from Pinners.
Being Resilient
Rebounding from setbacks and adversity when facing difficult situations.
Demonstrates composure and professionalism under difficult circumstances; recovers quickly from problems and setbacks; maintains productivity under stress or pressure, overcoming obstacles and learning from failures. 	
Courage
Stepping up to address difficult issues; saying what needs to be said.
Proactively and openly shares own ideas and points of view in service of an end goal, regardless of potential criticism.
Ensures Accountability
Holding self and others accountable for meeting commitments.
Takes ownership of work, including successes and failures; follows through on commitments, seeking input to resolve issues in times of ambiguity.
Decision Quality
Making good and timely decisions that keep Pinterest moving forward.
Demonstrates good judgment in routine, day-to-day decision making, determining whether to act independently or to escalate an issue to ensure quality outcomes. 	
Values Differences
Recognizing the value that different perspectives and backgrounds bring to an organization.
Behaves with sensitivity toward differences in norms, expectations, and ways of communicating; seeks to understand different perspectives and backgrounds. Works effectively with others who have different perspectives, backgrounds, and/or styles.
RESPONSIBILITIES


Impact
Delivers impact through hands-on technical work and has a track record of completing tasks, projects, etc that have impact to team and project goals.
- Hands-on technical work is the primary measure of impact.
- Has a track record of successfully completing assigned tasks with guidance.
- Contributes to team-level initiatives as a member of project teams.
Technical Excellence
Delivers production-ready solutions, follows and adapts best practices, and holds a high bar for technical quality.
- Delivers production-ready code with help from code reviewers.
- Delivers their own parts of production-ready ML solutions with sound technical choices under the guidance or supervision of others.
- Follows best practices in documenting their work, evaluating their models and making their work maintainable.
Mentorship and Knowledge Sharing
Uplevels the team through mentorship, feedback, and knowledge sharing
- Helps to onboard new hires/interns as they join the team and shares knowledge proactively.
- Documents their implemented solutions and maintains existing artifacts.

FUNCTIONAL SKILLS


Machine Learning Expertise
Has solid expertise in Machine Learning and is able to apply it to solving challenging problems
- Has solid knowledge of ML concepts and math foundations from textbooks or graduate school courses.
- Is able to apply the knowledge to solving Pinterest problems.

Drives succesful execution of projects through milestone setting, anticipating risks/uncertainties, and making improvements to team processes

- Open to new ideas and willing to embrace change in order to drive innovation and growth.

Effectively and proactively communicates to ensure alignment, and tailors their message to the audience
