JOB TITLE
IC15 - Senior Machine Learning Engineer
Role Summary
A Senior Machine Learning Engineer possesses comprehensive knowledge in their field, exhibiting leadership within their team. They are able to individually identify problems, design solutions, and lead projects to completion.
SCOPE OF ROLE


Scope
Takes an ambiguous problem, designs a solid ML solution and delivers the solution end-to-end to production. Leads junior engineers in the team and manages dependencies if some components are implemented by others.

Examples:
- Building a video watch-time prediction model end-to-end, including defining labels, creating training data, offline training and online inference, and offline and online evaluation.
- Developing a multi-objective optimization framework, including defining objectives and metrics, implementing optimization methods, and evaluating the methods offline and online.
- Solving the scaling problem for large model training and inference end-to-end, including defining success criteria, analyzing scaling issues, choosing and implementing the right methods, and evaluating the solution offline and online.


Level of Independence
Works independently, and is highly self-sufficient and capable of leading projects and initiatives of the corresponding scopes of their levels with minimal oversight.

RESPONSIBILITIES


Impact
Delivers impact through hands-on technical work and has a track record of completing tasks, projects, etc that have impact to team and project goals.
- While engineers at this level start to take on leadership responsibility, the primary measure of impact remains hands-on technical work.
- Has a track record of successfully leading and delivering projects or features which have substantial impact to team OKRs/business metrics, and are stable and maintainable in the long run.
Technical Excellence
Delivers production-ready solutions, follows and adapts best practices, and holds a high bar for technical quality.
- Delivers highly-maintainable production-ready code.

FUNCTIONAL SKILLS


Machine Learning Expertise
Has solid expertise in Machine Learning and is able to apply it to solving challenging problems
- Depth: Has rich experience in one area of the team’s ML domain and is able to lay out directions of a few sub-areas.
- Breadth: Has solid knowledge in the ML tech stack used by the team, and how different components fit together.
Efficient Execution
Drives succesful execution of projects through milestone setting, anticipating risks/uncertainties, and making improvements to team processes
- Leads projects involving multiple contributors, or individually manages a complex project themselves.
- Sets clear milestones, breaks down problems into manageable tasks for themselves and team members, delegates tasks if needed, coordinates updates proactively, and runs retros. Makes a best effort to estimate development time in order to ensure accurate timelines for their team, and adjusts timelines as new needs or complexity are encountered.
- Anticipates and resolves core technical uncertainties in design/approach.
- Identifies cross-functional dependencies and factors those into timelines and milestones.
- Proactively revisits the existing processes and/or practices of the team and puts improvements in place to address gaps.
Communication and Alignment
Effectively and proactively communicates to ensure alignment, and tailors their message to the audience
- Communicates with clarity, brevity, and focus, and tailors their message to an audience of their function, and team cross-functional partners
- Actively listens to others, asks clarifying questions, and leads team discussions, driving them towards conclusions and decision making.
- Influences stakeholders to help drive project level decisions
- Regularly communicates project progress, risks, and technical designs to team members and key cross-functional stakeholders
