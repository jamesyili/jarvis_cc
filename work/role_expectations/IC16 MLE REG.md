JOB TITLE
IC16 - Staff Machine Learning Engineer
Role Summary
A Staff Machine Learning Engineer is a leader who drives the technical solutions and strategic objectives for one or more teams. The impact of their
work is felt beyond their primary team
SCOPE OF ROLE


Scope
Delivers advanced ML solutions to ambiguous and complex problems by leading a team of engineers or individually solving technical challenges and coordinating across teams. Owns technical strategy and roadmap of an ML team. Remains hands-on with coding and modeling work.

Examples:
- Being a tech lead for core ranking of a discovery surface, responsible for objective functions, prediction targets, features and model architectures used by the surface
- Being a tech lead for a ML computation area, e.g., responsible for improving the scalability and efficiency of training or inference of ML models
- Laying out a team’s roadmap, identifying areas that need partner team support and influencing other team’s roadmap, and bringing in new ideas to improve ML solutions used by the team.
Level of Independence
Works independently, and is highly self-sufficient and capable of leading projects and initiatives of the corresponding scopes of their levels.
PINTEREST COMPETENCIES


Customer Focus
Building strong Pinners, partners, and/or stakeholders relationships and delivering user-centric solutions.
Identifies appropriate functional resources and support needed to meet current and emerging needs of Pinners, partners, and/or stakeholders; solicits user feedback on behalf of the team to understand problems and potential solutions.
Being Resilient
Rebounding from setbacks and adversity when facing difficult situations.
Remains objective and calm while taking a forward-thinking approach when faced with adversity. Grows from hardships, difficult experiences, and setbacks. Manages crises and volatile situations effectively.
Courage
Stepping up to address difficult issues; saying what needs to be said.
Confronts tough organizational issues and disagreements, delivering difficult messages directly and saying "no" when necessary.
Ensures Accountability
Holding self and others accountable for meeting commitments.
Identifies and monitors metrics and milestones for projects or products one leads. Designs feedback processes into the work. Holds self accountable for outcomes.
Decision Quality
Making good and timely decisions that keep Pinterest moving forward.
Promptly makes sound decisions while seeking appropriate context and vetting better alternatives when necessary. Synthesizes information, experience, and various inputs to determine the best course of action. Willing to make tough decisions, even if unpopular, after considering all relevant inputs and alternative courses of action.
Values Differences
Recognizing the value that different perspectives and backgrounds bring to an organization.
Interacts with others in ways that is sensitive to norms and expectations. Encourages others to stay open to, seek, and learn from different perspectives. Actively seeks out information and activities centered around a wide range of backgrounds, and viewpoints.
RESPONSIBILITIES


Impact
Delivers impact through hands-on technical work and has a track record of completing tasks, projects, etc that have impact to team and project goals.
- Engineers at this level take on increasing technical leadership responsibility while continuing to deliver impact through hands-on technical work.
- Has a track record of successfully guiding, leading and shipping projects that have substantial impact to team level OKRs, or other metrics critical to the organization.
- Leads technical projects which significantly improve the day-to-day lives of engineers, in a team or across multiple teams.
- Individual work products (documents, code, analysis) serve as examples for simplicity, quality and impact.
- Oversees and evaluates complex projects across one or more teams
Technical Excellence
Delivers production-ready solutions, follows and adapts best practices, and holds a high bar for technical quality.
- Delivers scalable, stable, and production-ready solutions that are maintainable over a long period of time.
- Ensures the ML solutions owned by their team are technically sound and is able to debug complicated ML problems and incidents with high ambiguity.
- Trusted code reviewer. Reviews complex or critical code changes across one or more teams.
- Uses knowledge of the development ecosystem to proactively identify and improve one or more teams area of ownership.
- Code serves as an example overall for quality, extensibility, and testing best practices.
- Identifies and drives programs that improve developer efficiency, testing, and documentation excellence by a step function across one or more teams.
- Is a role model in documenting their work, evaluating their models and making their work maintainable and leverageable. Sets the standard of best practices for their team, and ensures their team follows the best practices.
Mentorship and Knowledge Sharing
Uplevels the team through mentorship, feedback, and knowledge sharing
- Mentors and up-levels junior and senior employees across their function via direct feedback, regular 1-1s, code or analysis reviews, design or documentation reviews, pair programming, etc.
- Scales impact by focusing on developing senior employees that can in turn develop other junior employees.
- Reviews technical artifacts across one or multiple teams and takes responsibility to identify and address documentation gaps.
- Leads technical presentations attended by teams across an org, such as brown bags, all hands, technical design/analysis reviews.

FUNCTIONAL SKILLS


Machine Learning Expertise
Has solid expertise in Machine Learning and is able to apply it to solving challenging problems
- Depth: Is an expert of ML modeling or ML computation in a team and drives the vision of one important functional area of the team such as model architecture design and is able to unblock the team.
- Breadth: Is familiar with the ML tech stack across multiple teams.
Efficient Execution
Drives succesful execution of projects through milestone setting, anticipating risks/uncertainties, and making improvements to team processes
- Leverages resources to get priorities funded. Leads across one or multiple team efforts.
- Anticipates and mitigates potential sources of failure early.
- Sets clear milestones, delegates effectively, proactively coordinates updates, and runs retros. Proactively adjusts timelines to ensure they remain as accurate as possible.
- Creates needed processes to enable individuals to execute efficiently across their team(s)
Communication and Alignment
Effectively and proactively communicates to ensure alignment, and tailors their message to the audience
- Communicates with clarity, brevity, and focus, and tailors their message to an audience of their function, technical leaders, and cross-functional partners
- Influences stakeholders and obtains buy-in to help drive team level decisions
- Effectively communicates and creates alignment for team technical vision & roadmap to cross-functional audiences
- Addresses concerns by making alignment adjustments
