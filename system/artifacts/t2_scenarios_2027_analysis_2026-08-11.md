## What the three boards actually decide

All three boards share the same spine: James at UPP + Reflex with four directs; Alim takes Retentive Recs/pUIC (area 5-retrieval), UEB (area 8), and CLR (area 2); Daniel takes LWS (area 3) and Collection P13N (area 10). Only three things vary:

1. **The GenRet dial (area 7):** Alim (By Stages) or Daniel (Barbell, AnticipationCLR).
2. **The IB dial (area 9 — LLM for Recommendations):** Daniel (By Stages, Barbell) or Alim with Balaji and Ling (AnticipationCLR).
3. **Kim's seat** — fixable inside any board; her constraints (not-Daniel, not-100%-modeling, user-facing energy) point at an Alim-side user-facing seat everywhere.

The GenRet dial is really a disposition choice: GenRet is already the #1 CG on Homefeed, and the open question is how hard to press further investment. Under Daniel it gets conservatively compounded — higher floor, capped ceiling. Under Alim it gets bet bigger — higher ceiling, more variance, stacked on his other bets.

The IB dial, seen at area level, is a **seam selection**. Area 9 sits genuinely between the two teams: it *consumes exploration evidence* from Alim's side ("exploration supplies evidence; IB crafts the response") and it *reuses the collection substrate* from Daniel's side (area 10 is "the collection substrate Intelligent Boards should reuse: board representations, quality signals, ranking infrastructure, and evaluation practice"). Wherever IB lives, one of those two relationships becomes cross-team. By Stages and Barbell put the **evidence seam** across teams — an event/trigger contract, the kind of data interface the primer already names as crossing teams today. AnticipationCLR puts the **substrate seam** across teams — code-level reuse and shared evaluation practice, a deeper coupling that historically decays into reimplementation. That asymmetry is the strongest structural argument the new context adds.

## Costs every board pays — visible only at area level

- **Roderick fragments somewhere.** He TLs area 8 (Alim's in every board) but is also named in area 9's serving integration and as "overall backend lead, setting the architecture and technical direction across the portfolio" in area 10. By Stages and Barbell split his TL seat from both of his architecture seats; AnticipationCLR actually fragments him least (areas 8+9 co-located, only area 10 across). Every board needs an explicit answer — likely a standing architecture role that crosses the line through H2.
- **UEB's platform thesis crosses teams no matter what.** Area 8's own success test is its *second customer* — Intelligent Boards ("the second use case that tells us whether the abstraction is real"). In two boards the platform and its proving customer sit under different EMs; in the third, the platform separates from Recsplanation/collections instead. The Sep 8 pUIC-swap ramp ("the platform test") is evidence either way.
- **The LLM story splits somewhere in all three.** By Stages: GenRet + LLM-pUIC under Alim, IB's LLM application under Daniel. Barbell: GenRet + IB + Ling's pipeline under Daniel, LLM-pUIC under Alim. AnticipationCLR: LLM-pUIC + IB under Alim, GenRet under Daniel. Barbell comes closest to one LLM-applications center of gravity, and its remaining split (LLM-pUIC on two maps) is the one seam the primer already names as designed overlap.
- **Ling is load-bearing in the whole design.** The area texts name her three times: Board Ranker modeling + V3 line (area 10), the pUIC/LLM generation pipeline and evaluation foundation (area 9), and "the strongest emerging end-to-end LLM systems lane" (bench). Any board that splits her lane across orgs spends the org's scarcest asset. By Stages and Barbell keep her whole; AnticipationCLR moves her with IB while her Board Ranker lane stays behind — the 50/50 fragmentation problem, reborn at team scale. She joins Kim, Balaji, and Nima as a first-class placement criterion.

## Scenario 1 — By Stages (GenRet → Alim · IB → Daniel · everything gated)

**Pros.** Cleanest continuity with the July design Dylan approved — one anticipation owner, gated and reversible moves. Feeds Alim's growth arc and the hiring promise most directly. Daniel holds areas 9 + 10 adjacent — substrate reuse honored, Ling's lane intact, and (corrected view) a genuine portfolio: LWS engine + the LLM×Recs capability bet + a proven SSv2/WAU engine with real revenue. The evidence seam is the cheap one to run cross-team.

**Cons.** Alim's column stacks four bets against one engine that only arrives at settle — the concentration problem, unrepaired. GenRet leaves the scoring stack (serving and sizer synergies with LWS forfeited) and joins the bet pile, where Alim's disposition presses it harder — variance on top of variance. Daniel's *model-frontier* appetite is still the thing that went elsewhere: area 9's LLM work is application-and-evaluation, and his GenRet interest was declined. Seven people move onto a day-90 remote EM.

**2027 best case.** The retention thesis and the aggressive GenRet push both pay: model-pUIC lands late '26, LLM-pUIC recovers, RR moves WAU by Q2-27, GenRet impression share scales. Alim's org owns the company's retention narrative and he's M17-track by year-end. Daniel compounds quietly and completely — Board Ranker answers its SSv2 question, the notification WAU engine extends, IB's dogfood evidence converts into a funded second expression on his own substrate — M17 viable on impact. James sheds on schedule, ships the UPP exemplar, demos Reflex-absorbed-Shifu; M18 mid-to-late 2027.

**2027 worst case.** Both pUIC tracks are still troubled in Q1-27 *and* the pressed-hard GenRet bets misfire — in the same column. EVS dips across Alim's org; his day-180 recompute sours; the Search channel (Roberto, YiChin) is standing open, and he exits in spring. James inherits ~13 people and four stalled bets. Daniel is stable but frontier-starved — steady engines, declined interests — and while the IB capability persists, its next expression is unfunded in a cost year. The reorg narrative reads "the bets failed under the new EM." Materially softer than previously modeled (Daniel's side no longer collapses — areas 9+10 give him durable content), but the Alim-side concentration failure is intact.

## Scenario 2 — Barbell (GenRet → Daniel · IB → Daniel)

**Pros.** The only board where every team holds at least one engine and one bet — no column is a casino or a museum, so no single failure collapses a leg's narrative. Newly visible at area level: it **unifies the LLM-applications stack** — GenRet + IB + Ling's generation/eval pipeline + the collection substrate under one EM — with the already-designed LLM-pUIC overlap as the only split. GenRet lands on the scoring stack it shares serving economics with, owned by the most technically skeptical operator (who built the competing Best Pins system) — the paradigm gets an honest test. Daniel's M17 case completes: engines + a frontier bet + publications. Kim seats to her stated energy under Alim. Alim's R3 closes on day one via CLR. Areas 9+10 stay adjacent; Ling stays whole; the cheap seam goes cross-team.

**Cons.** It declines Alim's only stated lean — RecGPT — in his third week; survivable only as published criteria, never as preference between EMs. Daniel's span grows to four areas plus team on an EM who under-asserts overload — strain will be silent. And Alim's flagship bet acquires a cross-team dependency: LLM-pUIC leans on the generation pipeline Ling owns from Daniel's side. The backbone-built-once pitch to Dylan needs the UPP-style platform-consumed-by-legs framing to stay true.

**2027 best case.** The triple engine — LWS + CLR + GenRet — plus UPP carries the 4.5% SSv2; retention bets mature *behind* CLR's ballast instead of in front of an empty scoreboard. Daniel ships Board Ranker to its Recall targets, scales GenRet steadily, lands IB's next expression on his own substrate, publishes, passes every absence test — M17 by year-end, and he stays, because the org funded exactly what he asked for. Alim's leg posts its first retention wins mid-27 and earns growth at the gates; Chuxi's TL ramp survives because the seniors went engine-side. Both EMs promotable by EOY-27 — succession depth no other board produces.

**2027 worst case — contained, not cascading.** Daniel overloads silently: GenRet gets tended, not pressed; IB's evaluation discipline slips exactly where area 9 warns ("optimize the demo and call it quality"). Alim's bets stall while his LLM-pUIC pipeline dependency on Daniel's side becomes the friction ledger's favorite line. The RecGPT decline resurfaces as quiet grievance in the '27 planning cycle. The LLM serving investment gets built one-and-a-half times. But every leg keeps a working engine, nobody's column collapses, and the fixes — re-point GenRet, formalize the backbone under the UPP pattern — are available at the 2027 settle without a reorg. Worst case is a mediocre year, not a broken org.

## Scenario 3 — AnticipationCLR (GenRet → Daniel · IB → Alim with Balaji + Ling)

**Pros.** The purest expression of the anticipation thesis, and the new context strengthens its best argument: the **exploration→IB loop internalizes** — the flow the primer names as crossing teams today ("they flow into each other and do not currently sit together") becomes intra-team, and area 9's two load-bearing near-term risks, trigger quality and coverage, sit closest to the exploration evidence that feeds them. UEB's platform and its proving second customer co-locate. Roderick fragments least here. Balaji lands as Alim's Staff anchor per Daniel's own fit-read. And it most resembles Daniel's own written sketch — if his shed-line is a genuine depth-preference, this is the board he'd draw.

**Cons.** It re-concentrates people-and-bet risk on Alim — twelve people, eight moved in, every anticipation bet plus CLR plus a high-risk flagship. It puts the **substrate seam** across teams: board representations, quality signals, ranking infra, and evaluation practice that area 9 is supposed to reuse from area 10 now live in another org — the coupling most likely to decay into rebuild. It splits Ling's lane across managers — the org's best LLM-systems asset, re-fragmented. Daniel loses four originals early (the tool's own bad-optics banner) during the EVS window. And the IB transfer would land at the exact moment the sprint's dogfood-only evidence is being judged.

**2027 best case.** The loop compounds: trigger quality improves fastest because evidence and response share an owner; pUIC feeds IB feeds boards-retention; IB reaches coverage through UEB's config-level expansion (the platform thesis paying off in-house); CLR funds the scoreboard throughout. Balaji thrives at Staff scope. Daniel, at his smallest and deepest, ships the Recall targets and scales GenRet — his happiest 2027 *if* depth is his truth. Alim demonstrates M17-scale leadership a full cycle early, and James's M18 story is the boldest of the three: the anticipation super-org that moved company retention.

**2027 worst case — still the deepest, though softened.** The dogfood evidence lands thin; the next LLM×Recs expression goes unfunded in a cost year; the substrate reuse decays into parallel rebuilds that the cost review notices. Ling, caught between orgs a second time, becomes a flight risk. Alim's overloaded column misses retention *and* absorbs the IB reset; day-180 sours; the Search channel exit follows. The fold-back requires re-carving Daniel's team a second time in nine months, with EVS paying both times. The capability-outlives-product framing means this is no longer "inheriting a corpse" — but the org design that made one EM the single point of failure is what the worst case actually indicts.
