# The Pretrain-Finetune Paradigm in Recommendation Systems: Survey and Predictions

Where recsys borrowed the most powerful idea from NLP — and where it's going next.

## What to cover
- Why pretraining matters in recsys (data sparsity, cold start, transfer learning across surfaces)
- How Pinterest CLR does it: what's pretrained, what's fine-tuned, what signals each stage uses
- Survey of approaches: two-tower pretraining, sequential modeling, contrastive learning
- The UPP angle: how pretrain+finetune enables unified models across surfaces
- Predictions: will foundation models eat recsys the way they ate NLP? What's different about recsys that might resist that?
