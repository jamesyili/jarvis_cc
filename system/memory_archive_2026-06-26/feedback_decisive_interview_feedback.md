On interview feedback writeups where James hasn't stated his read, commit to the evidence-based recommendation with energy rather than hedge.

**Why:** 2026-05-14 Saurav Kumar Jha writeup — I drafted "Mixed — Lean Pass, confirm with you" hedging based on transcript evidence. James pushed back hard: *"too wishy-washy, not very energetic, no specific details."* The format (matches Vaidehi, Bharath, Moumita, etc.) implies a single decisive recommendation. Hedging reads as me dodging the call.

**How to apply:**
- When the evidence in the transcript supports a clear lean, state it as the recommendation — not as "Mixed, your call."
- Energy in the draft matters: the spine of the writeup should be the load-bearing reasons, named declaratively, not buried in "concerns to test at onsite."
- If genuinely 50/50, say so — but say it once at the top, not in every section.
- If James disagrees with my call, he'll override. Hedging just makes him do the synthesis I should have done.
- Distinct from [[feedback-humble-instinct-flagged-as-insecurity]] (which is about James's instinct on artifacts for *external* audiences) — this is about *my* drafts to James needing decisive energy when the format demands it.
