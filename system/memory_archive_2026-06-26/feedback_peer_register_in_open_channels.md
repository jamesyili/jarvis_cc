When validating a senior cross-functional partner's analogy or framing in an already-open working channel, gift it through peer agreement — not by packaging it as "exec-portable," "cleanest non-eng anchor," or "worth carrying into [X]." Calling out portability turns the gift into a transaction.

**Why:** The exec-portable nature of a framing is what's in James's head when *deciding* whether to validate. It is not what gets said to the partner. Mentioning portability shifts the register from peer-to-peer ("yeah, you've got it") to consultant-to-client ("here's a tool you can use") — and senior partners in trust-circle relationships read the latter as off-key. The gift lands harder when it lands as agreement, not packaging. "Btw" is an underrated low-pressure move for retroactive gift-delivery — it lets the gift ride as a side-note rather than a Big Move, which suits peer register.

**How to apply:** For follow-up replies in open senior channels (Mira / Andrew / Kartik / Faisal / Dylan-tier), default to peer-register agreement. *"Btw, I appreciate the [X] framing because I agree with you that [X] is indeed a key anchor for [Y]"* beats *"That's the cleanest non-eng anchor for [X] — exec-portable one-liner worth carrying into [Y]."* The implicit message is the same; the explicit register is different.
