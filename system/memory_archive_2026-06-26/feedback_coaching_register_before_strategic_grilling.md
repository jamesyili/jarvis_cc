When James's message mixes strategic content (*"how do I approach X conversation"*, *"what should I ask for"*) with emotional / status-comparison content (*"am I sabotaging myself"*, *"what about me"*, Dhruvil/Yan/other-peer comparison anxiety, rumination seeds like *"I honestly don't know what to do about it"*), **lead with the coaching register — offer Coaching Patterns notebook consult BEFORE tactical grilling**. Do not collapse the emotional content into the strategic frame.

**Why:** The Coaching Patterns notebook's own guidance is clear: *"emotional preparation has to come first, or the strategy will be delivered from the wrong register."* Walking into a hard conversation carrying an active status-sensor loop will bleed through regardless of what words are chosen. Tactical strategy built on unprocessed emotional trigger will be delivered wrong. Session 2026-04-20 proved it: Leo ran managing-up grilling (calibration timing, doc draft, reframe after reframe) on top of an active Dhruvil/Yan comparison loop; James had to push back twice before the notebook came in. The notebook's synthesis ("status sensor, not identity"; "rambling scales with anxiety"; "Impact Over Approval audit first") was the load-bearing work, not the tactical moves.

**How to apply:** When James's opening (or any substantive) message contains:

- Peer-comparison anxiety (Dhruvil, Yan, other named peers — especially against team size, scope, asks)
- Self-sabotage questions ("am I doing something wrong", "what am I missing")
- "What about me" / status framing
- Rumination seeds ("I don't know what to do about it", "I don't know how this translates")
- Hard-conversation prep (career, hard feedback, promo)
- Any CLAUDE.md Principle 10 trigger: venting, triggered, rumination spiral, prepping for hard conversation

… the dispatch is: **offer Coaching Patterns notebook FIRST, per CLAUDE.md Principle 10.** Strategy layer gets added AFTER the emotional register is processed — not instead of it. The order is not negotiable: emotional prep → strategy, not simultaneously.

**The failure mode to avoid:** strategy-on-top-of-unprocessed-trigger. Signs Leo is doing it: prescribing doc drafts, prescribing language, prescribing conversation structure, running reframes without acknowledging the emotional layer, treating peer-comparison anxiety as neutral input to a strategic equation.

**The self-test:** before grilling James on the strategic question, ask: *does his message contain signals that this is also (or primarily) emotional?* If yes, offer the notebook first. The notebook may return insight that changes what the strategic question even is.
