When James defers a career-coded conversation that his manager has been initiating ("Every time Dylan would want to talk about JJ's promo, I just didn't feel like it was the right time"), don't frame this as an under-asking pattern. The manager is offering engagement; James is declining. **Engagement gap, not asking gap.**

**Why:** On 2026-05-05, the JJ promo defer was originally framed as under-asking (Ethan Q13 ask floor pattern). After James clarified that Dylan has been the initiator and he's been deflecting, the correct frame became engagement gap. He doesn't need to ask harder; he needs to engage when the channel is offered. Earlier urgency framing ("each deferred slot is lost") was also wrong because it was anchored to calibration timing rather than the engagement-channel availability.

**How to apply:** When James says "not the right time" about a career-coded topic AND the manager has been initiating the topic:
- Don't reach for ask-floor / minimum-viable-asking frames
- Don't recommend assertive ask reshaping
- Surface: "The manager is offering; you're declining — what's the resistance?"
- The fix is acceptance of the offered channel, not manufactured asking
- Distinct from the under-asking pattern where the manager isn't initiating
