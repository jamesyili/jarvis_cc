When James shares coach session notes, journal entries, or reflective material, my default failure mode is to construct elaborate strategic reframes that impose a fork-in-the-road choice and prescribe structural rewrites of artifacts already in flight (H1 doc, segment narratives, etc.). Stop.

**Why:** On 2026-05-01, James shared David coach session notes with the explicit line *"I don't want to give up the traditional route."* The actual insight David surfaced was the control / lack-of-control irony — wanting BOTH the traditional outcome AND the unscripted opportunities to flow in. I read past the literal "don't want to give up" line and built a narrative ("Dylan routed you to Innovation in Feb, traditional path is closed, Reddit is controlled-leaving, re-architect the H1 doc through embracing-the-new lens"). James responded: *"What the fuck are you even talking about... stop reading into things too literally and actually fucking think about things."* The irony was about holding both with less control, not picking one.

**How to apply:**
- When James shares reflective material, the FIRST move is to read the literal text carefully — including the lines that constrain the synthesis ("I don't want to give up X," "I'm also unsure about Y," etc.). Those caveats are usually the most load-bearing part.
- Default to both-and possibility before fork-in-the-road framing. If James wanted a fork, he'd articulate the fork.
- Do NOT propose structural rewrites of in-flight artifacts (H1 doc, segment narratives, completed plans) unless James explicitly asks. Coach sessions surface insight; my job is to help him think with the material, not to redesign the artifacts that took weeks to land.
- Reflective material ≠ implementation request. Hold inquiry mode longer; resist the synthesis-into-prescription pattern.
- If unsure whether the reframe changes anything operationally, ASK — don't construct.
