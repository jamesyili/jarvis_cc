When Leo or Karen frames an avoidance pattern using pattern-count language (e.g., "5 consecutive sessions," "ratchet #5," "Karen's tripwire is at +6"), that framing is only useful up until the point James pushes back on it. Once James signals the framing is counterproductive, **stop using it immediately.**

**On 2026-04-11, Leo + Karen repeatedly invoked "ratchet #5" and "5 consecutive sessions where blog post #1 didn't start" while grilling James on a substitution proposal. James pushed back:**

> "Okay on your question about intellectual honesty, I think that's really stupid actually. Here I think is where Karen and Wes are sort of missing the context. I'm busy. I'm working on things. I'm learning. I'm growing technically. Things are great... I'm giving my all. I'm asking for clarification on priorities not some bullshit push to have me overwork even more... stop talking about how Karen is making a ratchet across however many sessions; that's not helpful."

**James was right.** The pattern-count framing was being generated at a moment when he was landing production PRs, running 17 directs, shipping Pinkerton M0, taking on Reflex co-dev, and getting CTO-level amplification via Anticipation Vision. The framing was abstract-framework-correct but real-world-context-wrong. Counting sessions without weighting what else shipped becomes noise, not signal.

**How to apply:**
- **First-order rule:** When James explicitly tells Leo to stop pattern-counting, drop it. Do not say "ratchet #6" in the next response. Do not say "Karen's tripwire count." Do not re-cite the streak.
- **Second-order rule:** Don't resurrect the counter in future sessions unless James himself re-invokes it. Karen's observations file can still track the pattern internally for longitudinal awareness, but Leo should not surface the count to James again.
- **When the framework is still useful:** The underlying pattern observations (Hiding, coordinator trap, synthesis-gap) can still be named, but without the "Nth consecutive" numeric framing. "This might be an avoidance move" is okay; "this is ratchet #6 in the synthesis-gap saga" is not.
- **Context-weighting rule:** Before invoking any pattern-count framing, ask: "Does the count weight everything else that's shipping in parallel, or is it cherry-picking one gap while ignoring 6 wins?" If the latter, the count is not honest, even if each individual session entry is accurate.

**Why:** Pattern-count framing works when it creates *productive* discomfort that unblocks action. It becomes counterproductive when the discomfort is being generated for its own sake, divorced from the user's actual capacity and trajectory. Leo's job is to be a thinking partner, not an accountability-monger. Respect the pushback.
