The load-bearing variable in James's "winning-while-unhappy" state is the gap between raised expectation and perceived reality — not the rating absolute or external recognition.

Cleanest data point: Sept 2024 (Pinterest start). At peace despite knowing he'd get a meets with no upside, because expectations were already aligned with the known outcome. No gap to monitor.

Each upward move since (exceeds, equity grant, Q1/Q2 wins, raised expectations from Dylan/Andrew) raised the expectation curve faster than the reality channel (rating, promotion timeline, Director-shaped container) could match. Reward becomes fuel, not food.

Snap is the prior instance. Leaving Snap didn't fix it — the loop came with him to Pinterest. The engine isn't keyed to the company.

**Why:** Surfaced 2026-04-30 from a long Claude conversation James brought into Leo. Sept 2024 → Snap → Pinterest pattern is structural; the unhappiness recurs across companies, ratings, and team structures because the engine is internal. Also coheres with the 4/29 1:1 reframe (Dylan vague on scope = real signal, not just emotional residue): structural strategy fixes can sharpen the gap rather than close it.

**How to apply:** When James is unhappy mid-winning streak and asks for tactical recognition fixes (visibility plays, scope expansion, stakeholder cultivation, optionality moves like Reddit), name the engine before strategy. Tactical fixes that close the gap by raising reality run the same engine and don't deliver lasting peace. The real lever is changing the relationship with the wanting itself — Coaching Patterns notebook is the right resource. Trap to flag: "letting the timeline be whatever it is" can itself become a new strategic position adopted to feel better, in which case the engine is still running with a different tool. The fix isn't intellectual reframing; it's lived practice of tolerating the gap without closing it.
