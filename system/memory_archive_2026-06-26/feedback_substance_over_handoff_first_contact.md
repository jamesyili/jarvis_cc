For first-ever direct exchange with co-author-tier seniors James is cultivating, lean toward substantive co-think (3-4 concrete options + parallel-thread links) over crisp relay-race-confirmation handoffs.

**Why:** 2026-04-28 Mira Steckel exchange. Wes Kao + Ethan Evans notebooks independently converged (4 of 5 major moves) on a v3 draft that closed with a relay-race confirmation ask ("does your team have what they need from Eng to take the next iteration?"). James chose his own version: substantive co-think, 4 concrete Design options, plus a link to a parallel thread he'd already opened in #anticipation-cupcake. Three-lens post-send review: Ethan 8/10 (cultivation hit), Wes 6/10 (BLUF/crispness miss), Leo 7/10. The deliberate tradeoff: relay-race optimizes for ownership clarity; substantive co-think invests in the channel itself. With brand-new senior contacts you don't yet have a working channel with, opening > closing.

**How to apply:** When drafting first-contact replies to Sr Director+ stakeholders who initiated direct contact, default to 3-4 concrete substantive options + parallel-thread links over relay-race-confirmation asks. Trade off ownership-clarity for channel-investment. Save the relay-race close for *ongoing/recurring* channels where ownership-routing is the actual goal. Two-step delivery (fast acknowledgment + delayed substantive reply) signals "taking this seriously" in a way an instant reply can't. Note: this means the cleanest single-message optimization (what notebooks tend to recommend) is sometimes wrong for the relationship objective.
