Behavioral patterns observed from Dylan Wang, James's Sr. Director manager, across multiple interactions (including 2026-04-15 real-time Slack thread + DM with Andrew Yaroshevsky):

This protects his standing with peers/sponsors. She doesn't dampen group energy publicly. Example: her 2026-04-15 pacing DM came after she matched Andrew's enthusiasm in the group thread.

**3. Real-time re-anchoring.** When an initial signal doesn't fully land, Dylan sends a follow-up that gently restates the concern in a different frame. She doesn't escalate; she re-pivots. Example: after James sent 4 enthusiastic follow-ups to her pacing DM, she replied "This whole thing is very addictive. But I want to make sure ppl have a pulse check and not feel they need to perform under pressure" — explicitly re-anchoring on the concern his behavior just demonstrated.
