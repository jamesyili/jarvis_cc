When James lands on a "humble" framing for high-altitude artifacts (exec-facing docs, career conversations, sponsor messages), grounded NotebookLM reviews from both Wes Kao and Ethan Evans frameworks consistently flag it as projecting insecurity rather than projecting altitude.

**Why:** Two observations within 24 hours.
- 2026-04-25e UPP §10: James drafted per-bet "pushback we want" itemization on a cross-org operational charter. Channeled review praised it. Grounded Wes Kao review flagged as Insecure Vibes (over-explanation projects defensiveness); grounded Ethan Evans review flagged as 70% Rule violation (Director altitude is decisive, not crowdsourcing). Same fix from two framework angles: drop per-bet itemization, replace with declarative proposals + section-level disagree-and-commit deadline.
- 2026-04-25f H1 career convo: James landed on "I'm here to learn what you need / where I can be useful" inverted-ask framing as the Director-altitude move. Grounded Wes Kao review (Q7 + Q15) flagged as overcorrecting into self-erasing — puts the cognitive load of diagnosing the business + mapping the career onto Dylan. Director move is OAV: observe gap, assert thesis, validate.

**How to apply:**
- When James shares a draft / framing / ask that reads as "humble" or "deferring to the room" at director-and-above altitude, do not just affirm. Flag the pattern: "This reads humble, but the pattern from 25e + 25f is that grounded reviews keep flagging humble framings as projecting insecurity. Want me to pressure-test it before you commit?"
- Specifically watch for: open-ended "what do you need from me?" questions to senior people; "I just wanted to share..." softeners on assertive content; per-bet "let me know what you think" invitations that should be declarative.
- The replacement pattern is OAV (Observe, Assert, Validate): bring a thesis, give them something concrete to push back on. Wes calls the trap "intellectual laziness — putting the cognitive load on the manager."
- Do NOT apply mechanically — sometimes humility is genuinely the right register (peer-to-peer, junior-asking-mentor, post-mistake). The trigger is specifically high-altitude artifacts where James is signaling he belongs in the room.
- Karen-level pattern: track recurrences in future sessions. Three+ instances = pattern is systematic enough to update James's coaching stance more permanently.
