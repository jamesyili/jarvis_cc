**Rule:** When James shares peer-friction triggers (Dhruvil-style preemptive moves, peer-comparison frustration, "X is pissing me off"), don't jump straight to tactical pushback drafting. First ask: *"what's the structural reality on the ground that hasn't surfaced in our context yet? What's already happening between you and the relevant adjacent stakeholder?"*

**Why:** James operates with substantial uncoded structural intel — existing partnerships, in-flight collaborations, real-world working relationships — that doesn't make it into context files until something forces it out. This intel often *already* contains the answer to the peer-friction question. Reactive trigger → forced articulation → structural reality surfaces. Once surfaced, the friction often dissolves: the trigger was peer-vocabulary; the reality is bilateral substance. Tactical pushback drafted on incomplete-context intel is at best wasted effort and at worst a defensive over-build that misreads the field.

**How to apply:**
- **Trigger condition:** James venting about a peer move (preemptive vocabulary-staking, joint asks routed through them, public framing), peer-comparison frustration, or "X is pissing me off about timing/style/etc."
- **First move (before drafting anything tactical):** *"Before I draft a response — what's already happening structurally between you and [the adjacent stakeholder being routed through]? Any in-flight work, existing partnerships, conversations already happening that we haven't named?"*
- **Then:** integrate the surfaced reality into the operating frame BEFORE the tactical response. The right reply is often "do less, but in your own bilateral register" rather than "engage on their thread harder."
- **Companion to:** `feedback_dont_over_fortify_on_sensitive_intel.md` (don't over-build defensive scripts on first-pass) — same family of patterns. Both say: pause, check the field, then act.

**Origin:** 2026-04-26 session. Dhruvil joint-Daniel email triggered James ("Dhruvil's kind of pissing me off with his timing"). The "what's already happening with Daniel?" question surfaced existing pUIC partnership between James's team + Daniel's TM + ATG, with offline lift landing and online experiments queued — plus Daniel's TL signaling mid-May for extensive planning, plus Daniel personally having said he wants to work with James. Once surfaced, Dhruvil's vocabulary play looked thin (he was trying to lay vocabulary on a relationship James had already been building) and the H1 doc's load-bearing operating frame shifted: Anticipation Foundations stopped reading as aspirational and started reading as already-in-motion-with-receipts. James dispelled the Dhruvil thread cleanly using the surfaced structural reality + April 3 consensus framing.
