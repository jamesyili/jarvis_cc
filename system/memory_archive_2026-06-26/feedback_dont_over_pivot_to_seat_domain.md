When James shares lived experience in a domain that maps to a target seat (e.g., integrity work when the seat is integrity), **don't make that domain the career through-line.** Lead with the underlying obsession (user experience, customer problems, ML calibration); name the domain as one chapter of that work, not the spine.

**Why:** Surfaced 2026-05-22 during OpenAI May 27 prep — twice in the same session. Story-spine drafts kept reaching for "I've been doing integrity my whole career" framings, anchored on FB civic + Snap Discover. James called it out both times:

1. *"this is not good. This is over-pivoting to integrity. Make it seem like I have really optimized for the user experience rather than just over-pivoting towards integrity."*
2. *"This is also kinda overdoing the integrity angle, right? This sort of means that all the recommendation systems craft I've picked up in the past three years or so is not really meaning anything, which isn't true."*

The over-pivot signal to a recruiter: *candidate trying to fit the seat* (rather than candidate whose career happens to include this domain). Also implicitly dismisses adjacent work — saying "integrity is my career" tells a recruiter the recsys craft is just engagement-flavored integrity, which collapses three years of substantive work.

**How to apply:**

- **Default through-line shape:** lead with the underlying obsession or craft (e.g., "user experience side of ML at scale," "preventing bad user experiences via classifiers"). Surface multi-dimensional examples (engagement growth AND quality reduction AND retrieval). Domain becomes implicit, not foregrounded.
- **Numbers do the credentialing.** Quantified wins (60% report drop, 30% YoY growth) carry the proof; framing should NOT be "look at my domain credentials."
- **Domain as familiarity, not identity.** "I have reps in [domain]" is calibrated; "I cut my teeth in [domain]" is over-claiming.
- **For "why this seat" bridges:** lead with the underlying career-direction pull (e.g., "upstream model layer altitude") and let the domain be ONE example of that altitude. The portfolio-opening move sits naturally inside.
- **Watch for the trap pattern:** when James shares a war story in the seat's domain mid-session, my instinct is to weave it into the through-line as the credentialing peak. That's the over-pivot. Surface the war story as ONE example of the broader pattern, not THE pattern.
- **Honor adjacent work.** When the seat is in domain X, never frame the candidate's non-X work as "the X-version applied to Y." That dismisses Y as derivative.
