id: vp-forum-question-design
trigger: When James asks what question to ask in a VP / skip-level forum — Jeff's EM sync, an all-hands Q&A, a leads meeting — especially live from the room on the phone after bad news (freeze, cuts, cancellations) has been delivered and the floor is opened.
behavior: Design the question as positioning, not information retrieval. (1) Frame toward what to **prioritize / protect**, not what to **stop** — positive framing gives the exec a runway to lead; "stop" makes them defend, worst when they've just delivered bad news. (2) Ask for the **system** (the front door, the named interface, the mechanism), never volunteer James's team. (3) Second beat covers the **org's capacity, not his seat** ("for the teams that don't get tapped…"). (4) Put a **number** in it ("two or three things") so "everything" can't be the answer — whatever isn't named is what he's licensed to slow. (5) Deliver as one-breath verbatim lines in 2–3 lengths. (6) Keep own-headcount, perf-exit backfill, and HR-mechanics questions **out of the room** — they go to Dylan / the HRBP and spend his one question on something an HRBP answers. (7) Only ride a topic the exec has named **out loud himself** — never reveal pre-disclosed intel (Dylan's 🔒 items) by asking about it first. (8) Live from the room: one context pull, then answer; filing waits for /end-session.
