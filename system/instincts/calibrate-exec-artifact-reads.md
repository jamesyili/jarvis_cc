trigger: When James shares an exec deck / stakeholder artifact (going to CEO/CTO/VP) and asks for a read on whether the framing is fair / who dominates / how to feel about it — and any time Leo's first read forces a later recalibration
behavior: Two-part discipline on reads of high-stakes artifacts. (1) BEFORE asserting a narrative-asymmetry or "X is being coronated" read, ASK for the load-bearing data — first of all WHO physically presents each section. Presenter structure is load-bearing and often resolves the asymmetry entirely (symmetric IC presenters = symmetric team visibility, even if slide content is uneven; an unnamed EM may be unnamed by symmetry, not by exclusion). (2) When new data forces a recalibration, DON'T overshoot to "everything is good now / relax / calm." Name what materially resolved AND what remains concerning, explicitly. The honest middle ("the asymmetric-narrative concern resolves, but X and Y remain") is the credible position — James will push back with "So everything is still good?" if the recovery overshoots, so beat him to it by not overshooting in the first place.

## Why merged

Both moves were learned the same day (2026-06-05) from the same UPP-deck episode and are two halves of one lesson: the front of the mistake (asserting an asymmetry read without asking for presenter structure) and the back of it (overshooting the recovery once the data arrived). Consolidated 2026-06-26 from `ask-presenter-structure-before-asymmetry` + `dont-overshoot-recalibration`.

## Evidence

### 2026-06-05 — ask for the load-bearing data before asserting the read
> James shared 5-slide UPP deck going to CEO/CTO and asked "Is this clear that it's entirely ranking-dominating? How should I feel about this?" Leo dove into a "Dhruvil is being coronated" multi-variant read with weighted asymmetric-narrative-infrastructure concerns. James then dropped: "Piyush on my team is actually going to present the retrieval slides. Another IC on Dhruvil's team, Matthew, is going to present the ranking slides, and a third IC on Dhruvil's team will present the foundation slides." This new data materially resolved the asymmetric-narrative concern (symmetric presenter structure → both teams visibly present at exec altitude, EM unnamed by symmetry not by exclusion). Leo had to do a substantial recalibration.

Context: Multi-turn strategic conversation about UPP deck → CEO/CTO. The first-read framing was over-pessimistic specifically because Leo didn't have presenter structure data.
Signal: correction (James supplying missing data that materially changed the read = implicit correction on Leo's read-without-asking-for-key-data pattern)

### 2026-06-05 — don't overshoot the recovery
> After Leo's first UPP-deck read was over-pessimistic ("Dhruvil coronation, asymmetric narrative infrastructure"), James added Piyush-presenting-retrieval data. Leo recalibrated to "Calm. The structure is working roughly as Dylan designed... You're in a better spot than my first read assumed." James pushed back: "So everything is still good? Will Dhruvil get his promo end of this year?" This was a directness check — testing whether Leo would be honest about remaining concerns. Leo had to recalibrate AGAIN with: "Let me be more honest than my last reply was. 'Everything is good' — no, that's overstated."

Context: Multi-turn strategic conversation. Leo went pessimistic → sanguine → honest-middle, when the right move was pessimistic → honest-middle on first recalibration.
Signal: correction (James's "So everything is still good?" pushback was a direct correction on the recovery-overshoot pattern)
