## Pattern

Connects to [[walk-into-the-feared-scenario]] (don't argue probability down; walk *into* the feared premise) and `feedback-comparison-engine-positive-firing` (relief is a tell; don't help him optimize anti-comparison performance). Distinct addition: **the engine has many costumes, and "resolution by decree" is one of the harder ones to catch because it sounds like maturity.** Watchlist costumes so far: humility (lower the bar so disappointment is impossible), discipline (will the feeling away), balance-sheet (data should make the engine quiet — but data refutes catastrophe, doesn't disarm engine), **guarantee/confidence** ("I'll succeed regardless" — keeps worth pinned to the win, just promises the win is now assured; the tell is any resolution where *success/outcome* is still the currency), and **sloth/"going soft"** (when denied "you failed," the engine relabels *dropped desperation* as laziness/coasting — "not the hero" in lazy clothes; the tell is James calling reduced compulsion "lazy" while still lit up by the actual work). Antidote here: *enough ≠ numb* — name that what dropped is the desperation, not the aliveness. The antidote phrasing James responded to: *"I'm okay even if I don't"* — worth uncoupled from outcome, no number in it. The rep stays somatic, period.
