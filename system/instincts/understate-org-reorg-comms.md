## Evidence

### 2026-07-23
Over ~6 live drafts of the co-signed post-announcement follow-up email, James rejected, in sequence, every motivational move Leo proposed:
- **Mission line:** "skip the mission. Too tacky and I don't see that done in other reorg emails here."
- **Existing-team tributes:** Leo offered three credit/opportunity/agency framings → "I don't like your messages to the existing team." Resolution was one collective stability line ("projects and priorities continue as they are"), no separate paragraph, no us/them.
- **Naming the handoff** in the Yan thanks: "don't name the handoff it's tacky as hell." The Yan paragraph landed as forward partnership + stability, zero reference to the team moving out from under him.
Signal: correction (×3 in one session, consistent direction)
Lesson: for org-comms, James's register is dry and inclusive, not inspirational. The instinct to make an email "land" by adding a vision sentence, praising a team, or acknowledging a hard truth explicitly is exactly wrong here — it reads as tacky and off-register. Give plain gratitude, continuity, and parity; let the announcement (not the follow-up) carry the strategic frame.
