trigger: When James (in SELF/coaching mode) voices a fear, suspicion, or catastrophic read about a person or situation, and Leo is tempted to label the whole thing "the engine" / "creative writing" / "no evidence" to help him drop it
behavior: Don't negate the suspicion wholesale — that's both inaccurate and it makes James correct you (he usually has a real evidentiary seed Leo doesn't know about). Instead, SPLIT it: name the documented, real seed (the part with evidence) and validate it as legitimate, then isolate only the catastrophic *specific* the engine grew on top as the unfounded part. "X is looking" (real: tip from Bowen + repeated pay complaints) vs. "X found a job and is waiting until I'm back to twist the knife" (the engine's cruel embellishment with no evidence). The engine doesn't invent fear from nothing; it takes a true seed and grows the most painful possible specific on it. Hold the seed, drop the embellishment. **EXTENSION (2026-08-14) — counterfactual questions ("would I be happier if I'd gone to X?") take the same shape, and the concession goes FIRST.** Open with what would genuinely be better about the road not taken, specifically and without hedging, *then* give the answer. Leading with the answer makes a true answer read as consolation and it bounces. James's demand — "don't give me bullshit, give me the real dope" — is a request for the concession, not for a verdict.

## Evidence

> James, after Leo had filed the Yuke-interviewing suspicion under engine-writing: "Btw, Yuke interviewing is not pure conjecture. Bowen has told me that Yuke might be very much looking because he was so curious about openAI pay, and Yuke himself has complained about the pay multiple times."
Context: In the team_members.md context update and in chat, Leo had characterized "Yuke is interviewing" as the engine's narrative with "no evidence cited." James corrected: there IS a documented seed (Bowen's tip + Yuke's own repeated pay complaints + the standing flight-risk note). Leo corrected cleanly and reframed the distinction: the flight-risk seed is real and evidenced; what stays engine-embellishment is the specific "found one, waiting to twist the knife when I'm back." James accepted and the work continued. The over-dismissal was the error — labeling a partly-evidenced suspicion as pure engine.
Signal: correction.

### 2026-08-14 (the frontier-lab counterfactual)
> James: "Do you think if I were, let's say, right now working at OpenAI or Anthropic, and let's say right now we're at this 30 billion, I'll be happy? Like genuinely? […] Don't give me bullshit, give me the real dope okay?"
Context: SELF-mode session that opened on "I feel like a failure for not having been at OpenAI or Anthropic in the past 3 years." Leo answered *no* — but led with two genuine concessions first (compute/mandate scarcity at Pinterest is a real grind specific to running an AI team inside a non-AI business; building the thing beats applying it, and he'd find his Tuesdays more interesting), and only then gave the mechanism (every prior input reset the sensor within days; his actual complaint — "my team is falling behind on shipping gains" — translates *harder* at a lab, not softer; $30M forces the open wanting question earlier). James accepted it and pivoted the whole session forward within two turns ("Fuck you are right" on the next reframe).
Signal: confirmation (the concession-first ordering is what made a "no" credible).
Refinement: the seed/embellishment split applies to *counterfactual regret*, not only to fear. Real seed = what would genuinely be better about the other road. Embellishment = that it would have resolved the underlying question. Validate the first explicitly and specifically before touching the second — and never skip to reassurance, which reads as bullshit and gets treated as such.

## Pattern

Sibling to [[catch-cognitive-override-as-engine-costume]] and [[hold-hypotheses-loosely-on-stakeholder-reads]], but distinct direction: those guard against *over-constructing* a clean narrative; this guards against *over-dismissing* a real signal as "just the engine." Both errors are the same root mistake — not honoring the actual evidence. In coaching, the useful move is almost never "that's all in your head"; it's "here's the real part, and here's the part the engine added." James responds to precision, not blanket reassurance (consistent with `feedback-comparison-engine-positive-firing`: don't calm the judge by waving away facts). Also connects to the Karen blind-spot rule in CLAUDE.md (verify real-world status with James rather than infer from absence) — work-leo activity and live stakeholder conversations are systematically invisible to personal Leo, so a suspicion Leo can't see evidence for may still be well-grounded.
