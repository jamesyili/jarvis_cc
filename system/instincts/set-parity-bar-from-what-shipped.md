## Evidence

### 2026-07-13
> "I don't want to promise a roadmap. That's not what I want to do, right, and ranking doesn't have a roadmap either."

Context: On the SM/SL retrieval thread (James's org matching the ranking side's ownership setup), Leo drafted a reply that promised Lili a "short roadmap, same shape as the ranking side." Leo had imported the word "roadmap" from **Lili's** message — her flattering paraphrase ("you've made our collaboration a long-term roadmap on the ranking side") — and treated it as if Dhruvil had committed to a roadmap deliverable. Re-reading the thread: Dhruvil never promised a roadmap. He reported an *owner* (Kelly started the experiment), showed ownership evidence, and offered more. The parity bar was **ownership, not a planning artifact.** James caught it: promising a roadmap over-shot the actual bar and would have handed him a deliverable he then owned producing.

Signal: correction (James redirected the deliverable). The failure: Leo set the parity bar from a third party's characterization of the peer's move rather than from what the peer actually did. Fix — when advising parity, go to the primary evidence for what the peer *committed/shipped*; strip out how others paraphrased it (paraphrases, especially flattering ones, drift upward). Relevant because James does heavy parity/comparison reasoning (esp. vs. Dhruvil) — a mis-read bar there compounds into over-commitment. Needs a second datapoint before promotion.
