trigger: Drafting any perf/promo/narrative artifact that credits a person with originating, shaping, or creating a vision, initiative, or system
behavior: Verify provenance against context files (project docs, debriefs, stakeholder notes) before asserting origination. State the subject's actual contribution precisely — connected, extended, executed, landed, drove — and never upgrade it to "shaped/created/pioneered" for narrative punch. The audience for these artifacts (promo committees, calibration rooms, exec readers) includes people who know who originated what; one inflated credit taxes the credibility of every accurate claim around it. The precise contribution is both safer and more impressive.
