## Action Plan (prioritized)

### P0 — start now
1. **Sodium <2,300 mg/day** (stretch: ~1,500–2,000). Single highest-leverage change —
   directly lowers urine calcium. Tactics: minimal soy sauce, no processed/cured foods,
   sauces on side, label rule >400 mg/serving = skip, home cooking with acid/herbs
2. **Zero sugary drinks / minimize fructose; red meat & shellfish ≤1–2×/wk; minimize
   beer.** Hits urine + serum uric acid AND triglycerides simultaneously

### P1 — maintain
3. Hydration ≥2.5–3 L/day (~85–100 oz) — already achieving, don't slip
4. **Normal dietary calcium (~1,000–1,200 mg/d) WITH meals — do not restrict.**
