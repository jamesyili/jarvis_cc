## 2. Landscape: what she'll actually face (post-SFFA)

- **Numbers:** Harvard Asian-American enrolled share 29.9% (class of '27) → 37% ('28) → 41% ('29). MIT ~40% → 47% in year one. Yale dipped then recovered to 30%; Princeton 27%; Stanford ~44% (check-all). Enforcement pressure is real and now runs *in favor of* Asian applicants (SFFA letters to Yale/Princeton/Duke; DOJ's 2026 UCLA action). Quiet reversion is unlikely by 2034.
- **The residual problem is sameness, not ceilings.** Trial record: Asian-American applicants had the highest academic/EC ratings and the lowest "personal" ratings; readers labeled undifferentiated files "Standard Strong" ("will need to fight it out with many similar to him"). Peer-reviewed work (Park et al., 6M Common App files) shows activity counts and generic leadership are the *baseline* for this demographic, not a signal.
- **Humanities positioning is genuinely scarcer:** humanities degree share fell by a third in a decade; consultant estimates put ~80% of elite applicants in STEM lanes (flagged as consultant math, but directionally consistent). A reading/writing/speech scholarly identity attacks the "familiar profile" problem head-on — while keeping math solid, because rigor floors still apply.
- **Testing is back everywhere** — every HYPSM/T10 school requires scores again (Princeton was last, from the 2027–28 cycle). For her cycle assume mandatory, rising-weight, and AI-proof-valued. Practical bar: 1550+ SAT is the no-questions zone for her demographic; below a school's 25th percentile is a hard headwind.
- **Bay Area docket density is real but not a quota:** she'll be read against the nation's densest pool of high-stat Chinese-American applicants. Geography amplifies the differentiation imperative; it doesn't cap her.
- **The demographic cliff won't loosen HYPSM** (elite demand is projected *up* ~14% even as CA's graduate cohort shrinks ~29% by 2041) — but it will make T25–T50 measurably hungrier, which improves the match/safety tier of her eventual list.
- **By 2034 expect verification-heavy formats:** AI essay-detection pushes weight toward interviews, proctored writing, and defended work (Caltech already runs an AI viva). A kid whose ability is *live and demonstrable* — competitive speech, extemporaneous argument — is positioned for exactly where the system is heading.
- **Heritage narrative:** post-SFFA, race is usable only as evidence of individual qualities (the Roberts carve-out). Bilingual rhetorical range feeding a speech/writing spike = good material. Generic immigrant-striving essay = commodified and pattern-matched. Related: admissions discounts AP Chinese from heritage speakers as path-of-least-resistance — keep Mandarin for family/cognitive value and as *material*, plan Spanish/French/Latin as the transcript language.

### The edge: memory → live articulation (added 7/18)

Her observed talent — memorize a lot, play it back articulately and fast — needs one strategic reframe: in an AI era, *recall itself* is worthless as a differentiator (it's the one thing every model does better), but **retrieval + synthesis + live delivery under time pressure** is scarce, durable, and increasingly what elite evaluation formats measure (§2: interviews, vivas, proctored/defended work, Caltech's on-camera research defense). So train the composite, not the component:

- **Now (4th–5th):** memorized-delivery reps inside the speech academy — Declamation (delivering a great published speech from memory is literally a competition event), poetry recitation, and retell/summarize games at home ("teach us the book at dinner"). Low stakes, already fun for her.
- **MS (6th–8th):** Impromptu and early Extemp at academy/junior tournaments; **History Bee** (International Academic Competitions runs elementary + MS divisions — a memory-forward competition that rewards exactly her profile ⟨verify local availability⟩); quiz bowl (NAQT MS) as an option.
- **HS:** **Extemp is the purest expression** — 30 minutes' prep, current-events file in her head, 7-minute analytical speech. Poetry Out Loud and Model UN are adjacent outlets.
- **Watch-for (the honest one):** in an achievement-anxious kid, a playback talent can quietly become an applause treadmill — performing for approval, reciting for grades. Keep the family frame "share something cool you know," never "show them how smart you are." The creative build below is the structural counterweight: generation over reproduction.

Consultants come later:

**What the evidence says** (full vetting detail in `research/counselors_2026-07.md`): the only RCT (Avery/NBER) shows expert counseling shifts list construction and enrollment choices — *not* measured application quality; essay polish correlates with income more than with admission; no independent data shows consultants move admit odds at all. Every firm's published acceptance rate is a selection effect. So: buy list-strategy, sequencing, activity *editing*, and parent-child buffering — don't buy manufactured profiles or concierge labor.
