## Narrative + KPI as a leadership combination during cultural change

Lenny (00:11:28): Is there anything else you took away as a leader from watching that shift, that you bring to making change, transitioning people to working in a different way?
Tim Holley (00:11:36): Definitely focus on a clear KPI that the teams can rally around. That's one. The other is... And I respect Josh immensely on his ability to tell a really clear narrative and use that consistently over time. The old adage of you need to say something three times before people understand it. I would wager you need to say it another three times before they internalize it. And having that be part of the day-to-day conversation, it seems like such a small thing, but it adds up to having clarity on goal, the KPI point, and then clarity on why, the narrative point. If you can marry those two things, I think that's an incredibly powerful combination.
