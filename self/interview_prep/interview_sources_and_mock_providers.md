### What would make an external evaluator worth considering later

Not a logo or a title. For a future paid calibration session, ask for recent experience evaluating the relevant **role, level and technical domain**, a sample of substantive feedback, and willingness to explain a disputed technical judgment. A single observed session should establish whether they can probe depth and distinguish an incorrect answer from a defensible alternative. This is a proposed screening standard, not a finding that any provider above meets it.

## 4. How Leo should use the material

Proposed approach for the next phase; no new evaluator system or question bank has been built in this task.

1. **Preserve provenance.** For each report, capture company, team, role/level, interview date, publication date, original URL, access limit, and whether it is firsthand, edited, aggregated or reconstructed. Keep the actual follow-ups distinct from the writer's advice and guesses about rejection.
2. **Deduplicate before inferring a pattern.** A Chinese original, an English translation and a Reddit repost may be one account. Several copies do not establish a recurring interview practice.
3. **Match the role.** An inference-infrastructure SWE problem can supply a technical exercise without defining an ML EM's entire loop. Preserve contradictory accounts rather than averaging them into a fictional standard interview.
4. **Ground the grading separately.** Before a mock, check the relevant technical claims against papers, engineering references and calculations or runnable code where appropriate. During feedback, identify the specific answer, the issue, the evidence and an acceptable alternative. Mark anything not verified.
5. **Test transfer, not recall.** Use an unseen variant with changing constraints and deeper follow-ups. A memorized answer to a reported question is not sufficient evidence of depth.
6. **Keep the claim honest.** Leo can deliver demanding practice and specific evidence-backed criticism. This research does not certify equivalence to an experienced frontier-lab hiring committee or support reliable offer probabilities.

## Research scope and remaining gaps

Research covered English and Chinese company/role searches; exact remembered names; original forum examples; official provider service pages, booking entry points and availability notices; and first-party process/technical references. Chinese discovery also checked Nowcoder, Zhihu and LeetCode.cn without establishing sufficiently relevant original OpenAI/Anthropic reports to shortlist. That is a search result, not a claim that those sites contain none.

Generic commercial summaries without traceable candidates were deprioritized. Sites advertising covert interview assistance were excluded from the directory. No account creation, private-group access, paid retrieval, access-control bypass or outreach was performed.

The source rows and linked examples record the publisher/author, visible dates and access evidence without creating a duplicate bibliography. Where no date is given, it was not established. Provider qualifications, current appointment slots, hidden report bodies and the labs' internal rubrics remain unverified. This is a completed public-source directory, not an audit of paid content or coaching quality.

Research stopped after role-relevant reports, distinct provider types and important contradictions were established; further broad searches were predominantly repeated or weaker material. Recheck access and availability before spending money, and refresh interview reports against the specific team and recruiter instructions when an actual loop is scheduled.
