## Adjacent options (the composite-training layer)

These train the same edge — live delivery, improvisation, synthesis under pressure — in lower-stakes formats. Per the plan, these are swap-ins inside existing lanes, not additions.

1. **Peninsula Youth Theatre — "Exploring Improv" (ages 9–11).** Studio at 2510-A Old Middlefield Way, Mountain View (~5–10 min). Improv is creative generation + live articulation in one package — the cleanest single adjacent fit, and it feeds the creative-thinking goal directly. Fall catalog says "coming soon" (winter classes covered ages 5–13, so grade-4 coverage is their norm ⟨verify fall listing⟩). **Do now:** mailing list + email Caitlyn (Education Program Manager, caitlyn@pytnet.org, 650-988-8798).
2. **History Bee — Elementary Division (International Academic Competitions).** Confirmed: a parent can register directly (free Sponsor Portal account; homeschool/individual entrants explicitly supported). Free 20-min online qualifying exam any time mid-Sept–April → top half advance to in-person buzzer Regional Finals ($47) → Nationals. NorCal precedent: Dec 2025 event at Central Park, Santa Clara (~15 min). Memory-forward competition that rewards exactly her profile; the live-delivery value is at the buzzer stage. ⟨verify 2026-27 NorCal dates + whether the Bee (individual) runs locally vs Bowl-only, when the fall schedule posts⟩. **Do now:** create the free sponsor account.
3. **Palo Alto Children's Theatre** (city-run, 1305 Middlefield Rd, ~15 min). Classes ages 3–18 including playwriting/acting; their site blocked automated reads, so fall 2026 class list needs a human browse or call (650-463-4930) ⟨verify⟩. Strongest non-PYT theater-class candidate.
4. **Theater production route** — Sunnyvale Community Players' junior show (Shrek Jr, ages 8–18, season starts September, audition-based ⟨verify dates⟩); Los Altos Youth Theatre is production-centric with no fall classes for 9-year-olds found. Bigger commitment, memorized-delivery-rich; only if she's drawn to stage over class.
5. **Odyssey of the Mind / Destination Imagination — parked.** No existing elementary team found in/near Mountain View (nearest OotM Div I team seen: Tru School, Palo Alto). Joining means *starting* a team (~$385 OotM fees + coach; $165 DI + background-checked manager). Against the additions-displace rule, that's a heavy lift for marginal gain over improv + speech academy. Revisit only if a Challenger parent group materializes.
6. **Storytelling/recitation gap is real.** Poetry Out Loud is HS-only; county library contests are 12+; no elementary recitation program or competition exists locally. Coverage comes from the home rep ("teach us the book at dinner"), CommAcad/GSA interp-style classes, and Declamation events once she's in an academy circuit.

**Dead ends, for the record:** ComedySportz San Jose (HS+ only), Made Up Theatre Fremont (12–17), BATS Improv (SF, out of radius), JKYog Gavel Club Sunnyvale (11+).
