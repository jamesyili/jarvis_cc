# What Self-Improvement Means in the World of AI

How to gain judgment, get better at systems thinking, and work with others — when AI can do most of the legwork.

## What to cover
- The collection trap: building systems that collect knowledge vs actually learning (personal experience)
- What "judgment" actually is and why AI can't shortcut it
- Systems thinking: how to develop it when AI handles the details
- The human skills that compound: collaboration, influence, taste, knowing what to build
- A framework for using AI as a learning accelerant, not a learning replacement
- Honest reflection: what I've gotten right and wrong in my own journey

## Why I'm writing this
This is the most personal one. Karen's been holding up a mirror on exactly this pattern — I've been building elegant infrastructure instead of doing the hard synthesis work. This post IS the synthesis.
