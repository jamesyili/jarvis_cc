Christian Idiodi (00:17:35): The holy grail of product walk is really a reference customer. This is somebody that has used your solution or your product, loves it enough to tell people about it. The ultimate definition for me today of the love that somebody has for our product is they're willing to put their reputation on the line by telling people about it.
