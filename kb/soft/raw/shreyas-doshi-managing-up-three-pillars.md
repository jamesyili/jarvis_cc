In this masterclass, Shreyas Doshi breaks down the essential components of managing up effectively as a product leader. He emphasizes that while his personal approach evolved over his career, mastering these three pillars is crucial for success:

1. Constant Communication & Reassurance (0:02 - 3:00): You must actively keep your management informed about the state of your product. Even if the numbers seem obvious, don't rely solely on them; use one-on-ones and status updates (async or sync) to over-communicate your wins and maintain visibility, as managers often have limited bandwidth to track everything.

2. Strategic Escalation (3:01 - 7:07): Doshi warns against the trap of having "high agency"—believing you can solve every problem alone. He admits that he historically under-escalated, which prevented his managers from understanding the true difficulty of his work. Proactively sharing your frustrations and specific challenges gives your leadership the necessary context to support you, especially when your managers are less experienced in product management than you are.

3. Strategic Alignment (7:08 - 11:30): Aligning your product goals with your manager's and the company's priorities is essential. This involves actively soliciting what your leaders are currently worried about and, when necessary, realigning your team's focus. Doshi notes that while this can sometimes feel like "signaling" or require shielding your team from organizational churn, finding the right balance of alignment is a key driver for career progression.
